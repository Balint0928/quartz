This is a special form of the [[Help Action]].
You attempt to stabilize a creature in the [[Dying]] condition. You must succeed a Medicine check against their current Death Saving throw DC. If you succeed, the creature becomes stable, and stops rolling Death Saves. However, the creature, now not actively dying, passes out from shock and becomes unconscious instead. Meaning, they can no longer take actions, bonus actions, reactions, movements etc.. until they are either healed or they take damage. 

If a stable creature takes damage, they take [[Exhaustion]] as per the dying rules and resume rolling death saves.
