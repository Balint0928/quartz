You are so sure of your movements that you are unaffected by adverse conditions. When you roll a check for jumping (Acrobatics or Athletics), Dancing (Performance) or any contested checks against being [[Grappled]] or knocked [[Prone]]. You can, instead of rolling, take 10 as a result on the d20.
