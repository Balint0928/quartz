# Description
Creatures native to the Sleepless Sands. These large worm-like creatures dwell beneath the dunes of the desert, searching for their next prey.  Using their long antennas they can sense the vibrations of the sand. The creatures have two really small black eyes as well, that are hard to see. The worms are ambush predators, utilizing quick hit-and run tactics. Juveniles often hunt in packs, but older specimens prefer to hunt alone. The oldest of Sultan Worms are capable of reaching gigantic proportions. These ancient worms usually tend to live a more sedentary lifestyle, and hibernate for long periods of time. When they awaken, sand shifts, earth rumbles, and nothing stops their destructive feast.

The Sultan Worms move fast beneath the sands, and use their hardened shell plating to absorb blows. Their sharp teeth and strong jaws allow them to drag their pray beneath the sand where they devour them. The worm's tail also sports a stinger, that can produce a potent poison. As the worm becomes older, the poison becomes deadlier, capable of paralyzing the victim.

The Sultan Worms of the desert have a color between white and dark yellow or even pale red.
