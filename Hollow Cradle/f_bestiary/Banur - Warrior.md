# Description
A baboon-like, primitive sapient creature native to the mountainous and desert regions of Iqor. The Banur have long limbs, and a mangy mane around their head and neck. Their fur is mostly dark brown with white or grey spots and lines. They are harshly territorial, and able to create crude weapons and even armor. They make their homes in caves and abandoned ruins of the continent. Some of them are even capable of performing primitive levels of magic.
