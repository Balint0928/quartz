# Description
A creature of many legends, these winged serpents of the sky command awe and terror in equal measure. Their snake like body shimmers from heat, their leathery wings bat off smoke from their S-shaped body, as the creature glides through the scorching skylines of the Sleepless Sands. It is capable of heating up its own body to scorching temperatures, setting creatures ablaze from afar if it feels threatened, or it's territory is invaded. Despite its deadly nature, this magical beast is a herbivore, just a really stubborn one. Feared by humans and Alayyi alike, these gliders became a symbol of the resourcefulness, and fighting spirit of the people of the sands. Typically covered in greenish- yellowish scales, rare specimens were spotted sporting golden scales, that sparkle in the sunlight.
