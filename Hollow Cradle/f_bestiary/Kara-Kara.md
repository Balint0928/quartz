# Description
These large, cave dwelling predators inhabit the underground caverns in the Sleepless sands. When they hunt, they often leave their lairs, to hunt on the surface. They use echolocation to find their prey, and give off a signature screetching sound that drives fear into them. This sound is what they got their name from "Kara-Karaaaa!". Then they swoop down on their target, and use their razor sharp talons and sharp pointy theeth to drain their victim of blood, and devour their flesh. The Kara-Kara appear as large bat like creatures, with many lion like features, such as a mane that runs along their back, muscular hind legs, and a long tail that ends in fur. They have large bat like ears, and small beady eyes and leathery wings attached to their fore legs.
