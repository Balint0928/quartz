# Description
The quadruped avian species known as Khakut is a widely domesticated and used mount and beast of burden in the Sleepless Sands. These majestic, large, four legged creatures, have long feathered tails, and flat heads. They also have small feathered wings that are incapable of granting flight to these birds. Their fore legs end in blunt claws allowing them to dig in the sand, and their muscular hind legs end in hooves, allowing them to move fast, and deliver attacks to any predators. The birds have deep orange colored large eyes, and short but wide and strong beaks. They are able to travel quickly and easily on sand. They come in mostly grey color combinations, ranging from dark almost black feathers to, on rare occasion, even stark white.
