# 2nd Level

| Type         | Stat                           |
| ------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Casting Time | Action                         |
| Range        | 15 feet                        |
| Components   | V, S, M (a shriveled tentacle) |
| Duration     | Instant                        |
You summon a large number of tentacles and slugs from your palm to strike a creature within range. Make a melee spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 3d6 bludgeoning damage. If you score a critical hit, the target is also [[Stunned]] until the start of your next turn.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 3rd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 and range increases by 5 feet for each slot level above 2nd.
