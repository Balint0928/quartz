# 2nd Level

| Type         | Stat                        |
| ------------ | --------------------------- |
| Casting Time | Action                      |
| Range        | 60 feet                     |
| Components   | V, S, M (a sliver of glass) |
| Duration     | Conc. up to 1 minute        |
You conjure spinning daggers in a 5-foot Cube centered on a point within range. Each creature in that area takes 4d4 Slashing damage. A creature also takes this damage if: 
- It enters the Cube  
- Ends its turn in the Cube 
- If the Cube moves into its space 

**A creature takes this damage only once per turn.**

On your later turns, you can take an action to teleport the Cube up to 30 feet to a place you can see.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot:** The damage increases by 2d4 for each spell slot level above 2.
