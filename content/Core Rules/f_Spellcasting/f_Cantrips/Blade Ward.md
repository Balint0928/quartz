# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat     |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Casting Time | Reaction |
| Range        | Self     |
| Components   | V, S     |
| Duration     | 1 round  |
When you are hit by a weapon attack dealing bludgeoning, slashing or piercing damage, you can use your reaction and trace a sigil of warding in the air. You have resistance against the damage of the triggering weapon attack, that equals your spellcasting ability modifier. You can only benefit from this cantrip if you are not wearing medium or heavy armor, or a shield.
