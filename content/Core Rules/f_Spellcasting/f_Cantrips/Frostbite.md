# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat    |
| ------------ | ------- |
| Casting Time | Action  |
| Range        | 60 feet |
| Components   | V, S    |
| Duration     | Instant |
You cause numbing frost to form on one creature that you can see within range. The target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes 1d6 cold damage, and it gains weakness 1 to Bludgeoning damage until the start of your next turn.

At 5th level, the Cold damage increases to 2d6, and the Weakness gained to 2.
