# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat                  |
| ------------ | --------------------- |
| Casting Time | Action                |
| Range        | Self (10 - foot cube) |
| Components   | V                     |
| Duration     | Instant               |
You let out a horrible scream. Each creature in a 10- foot cube originating from you, that can hear you, must succeed on a Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 1d6 psychic damage, and is [[deafened]] until the end of its next turn.

The Psychic damage increases to 2d6 at 5th level.
