# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat    |
| ------------ | ------- |
| Casting Time | Action  |
| Range        | 30 feet |
| Components   | V, S    |
| Duration     | Instant |

You spray toxic mist at a creature within range. The creature must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 1d12 poison damage.

The poison damage increases to 2d12 when you reach 5th level.
