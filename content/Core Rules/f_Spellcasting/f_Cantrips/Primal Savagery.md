# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat    |
| ------------ | ------- |
| Casting Time | Action  |
| Range        | Self    |
| Components   | S       |
| Duration     | Instant |
You channel primal magic to cause your teeth or fingernails to sharpen, ready to deliver a corrosive attack. Make a melee spell attack against one creature within 5 feet of you. On a hit, the target takes 1d10 acid damage. After you make the attack, your teeth or fingernails return to normal. If your attack is a critical hit, reduce the AV of their armor by 1.

The Acid damage increases to 2d10, and the AV reduction to 2, at 5th level.
