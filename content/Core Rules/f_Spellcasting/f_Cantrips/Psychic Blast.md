# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat    |
| ------------ | ------- |
| Casting Time | Action  |
| Range        | 120 ft. |
| Components   | V, S    |
| Duration     | Instant |
A beam of psychic energy streaks toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 psychic damage and subtract 1d4 from the next saving throw it makes before the end of your next turn.

The spell creates one more beam when you reach 5th character level. You can direct the beams at the same target or at different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each beam.
