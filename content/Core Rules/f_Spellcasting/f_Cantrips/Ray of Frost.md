# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat    |
| ------------ | ------- |
| Casting Time | Action  |
| Range        | 60 feet |
| Components   | V, S    |
| Duration     | Instant |
A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, it takes 1d8 Cold damage, and its movement speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

The Cold damage increases to 2d8 at 5th level.
