# Cantrip Level

| Type         | Stat    |
| ------------ | ------- |
| Casting Time | Action  |
| Range        | 60 feet |
| Components   | V,S     |
| Duration     | Instant |
You hurl a bubble of acid. Choose one creature you can see within range, or choose two creatures you can see within range that are within 5 feet of each other. A target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or take 1d6 acid damage. If a target wearing armor made of metal fails this save by 5 or more, their AV is also reduced by 1.

This spell's damage increases by 1d6 and the AV reduction increases by 1, when you reach 5th level.
