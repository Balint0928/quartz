Each of the casting classes draw their power from a different Wellspring of Aether. The wellspring determines your class spell list. By default these classes are connected to the different wellsprings.

# Arcane Wellspring
- [[Bard]]
- [[Wizard]]
- [[Sorcerer]] - Depends on your [[Bloodline]] choices, but by default, 5e spells of the sorcerer spell list all belong in this wellspring.

# Occult Wellspring

# Primal Wellspring

- Default 5e Ranger spells
