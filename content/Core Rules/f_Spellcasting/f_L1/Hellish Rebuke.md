# 1st Level

| Type         | Stat                                                                                                              |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Casting Time | Reaction, which you take in response to taking damage from a creature that you can see within 60 feet of yourself |
| Range        | 60 feet                                                                                                           |
| Components   | V, S                                                                                                              |
| Duration     | Instant                                                                                                           |
The creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by otherwordly flames. It makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d10 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Upcasting:** The damage increases by 1d10 for each spell slot level above 1.
