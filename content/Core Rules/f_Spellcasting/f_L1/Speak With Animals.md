# 1st Level

| Type         | Stat             |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| Casting Time | Action or Ritual |
| Range        | Self             |
| Components   | V, S             |
| Duration     | 10 minutes       |
For the duration, you can comprehend and verbally communicate with Beasts.

Most Beasts have little to say about topics that don't pertain to survival or companionship. The knowledge and awareness of many beasts is limited by their intelligence, but at minimum, beasts can give you information about nearby locations and monsters, including whatever they can perceive or have perceived within the past day. You might be able to persuade a beast to perform a small favor for you, at the DM's discretion.
