# 1st Level

| Type         | Stat                              |
| ------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Casting Time | Action                            |
| Range        | 60 feet                           |
| Components   | V                                 |
| Duration     | **Concentration**, up to 1 minute |
You awaken the sense of mortality in one creature you can see within range. A construct or an undead is immune to this effect. The target must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or gain the [[Frightened]] condition of you until the spell ends. The [[Frightened]] target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Upcasting:** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 1. The creatures must be within 30 feet of each other when you target them.
