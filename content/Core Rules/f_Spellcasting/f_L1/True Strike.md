# 1st Level

| Type         | Stat         |
| ------------ | ------------ |
| Casting Time | Bonus Action |
| Range        | Self         |
| Components   | S, M         |
| Duration     | Instant      |
Guided by a flash of magical insight, you can ensure your next attack to strike true. The next attack roll you make before the start of your next turn, gains advantage.

**Upcasting:** Casting this spell from a 2nd or higher spell slot, allows you to ignore the target's benefits from half and three quarters cover, or being obscured. You also ignore any negative modifier to the attack roll.
