# 1st Level

| Type         | Stat                                |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| Casting Time | Action or Ritual                    |
| Range        | Self                                |
| Components   | V, S                                |
| Duration     | **Concentration**, up to 10 minutes |
For the duration, you sense the presence of magical effects within 30 feet of yourself. If you sense such effects, you can take a check action to see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bears the magic, and if an effect was created by a spell, you learn the spell's school of magic.

The spell is blocked by 1 foot of stone, dirt, or wood; 1 inch of metal; or a thin sheet of lead.
