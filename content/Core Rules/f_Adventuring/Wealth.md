There are five price categories for goods and services

**Inexpensive**
Things that common people buy. A simple meal or drink. Pen and some paper. A cheap book. Common household items.

**Moderate**
Things that common people buy, but not too often or in great quantities. A small piece of furniture. An expensive meal. A new outfit.

**Expensive**
Things that would strain the finances of a common person. A major piece of furniture. A very nice outfit. Cost of long distance travel. Simple lodgings for an extended time.

**Very Expensive**
Mostly out of the reach of common people, except for special occasions. Fancy jewelry. Luxury furnishings. 

**Exorbitant**
Something only the rich can afford. A nice house. A small sail boat. Extremely expensive jewelry or art pieces.

These categories work as powers of 10. A **Moderate** item is 10 times more costly than an **Inexpensive** item. An **Expensive** item is 10 times more costly than a **Moderate** item, so 100 times the cost of an **Inexpensive** item, and so on and so forth.
