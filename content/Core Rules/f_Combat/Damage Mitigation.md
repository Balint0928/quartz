When you are successfully hit by an attack or similar damaging source, you might have several sources of damage mitigation. The order of their application is as follows:

- Temporary HP goes first.
- Any special sources of mitigation that is not specified to apply before or after something else. (example: [[Battle Hardened]])
- [[Armor Value]]
- [[Resistances and Weaknesses|Resistances]]
