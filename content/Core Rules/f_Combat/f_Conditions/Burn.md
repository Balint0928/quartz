A burning creature or object takes 1d4 Fire damage (if not specified) at the start of each of its turns. As an action, you can extinguish fire on yourself by giving yourself the [[Prone]] condition and rolling on the ground. The fire also goes out if it is doused, submerged, or suffocated. Another creature can also use an action to douse the flames.
