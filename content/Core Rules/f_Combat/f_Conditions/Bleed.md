When a creature takes Bleed damage, the damage is only realized at the start of its next turn. Bleed damage doesn't stack with other Bleed sources, unless the effect specifically states so. At the start of an affected creature's turn, they take an amount of damage that equals their current Bleed counter. This damage cannot be reduced by [[Armor Value]], or effects like [[Battle Hardened]], and is not affected by physical damage resistance.

**Removing bleed:**
- An affected creature rolls 1d20 at the end of each of its turns. On a 15 or higher, all Bleed is removed.
- An affected creature or a different creature within 5 feet can use its action to attempt a DC 15 medicine check to stop the bleeding. This consumes a charge of the [[Healers Kit]].
- Magical Healing removes all stacks of Bleed.
- Bleed is also removed on its own after a time deemed appropriate by the DM. This is usually the end of the combat.
