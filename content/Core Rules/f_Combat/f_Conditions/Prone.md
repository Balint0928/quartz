While you have the Prone condition, you experience the following effects:

- Your only movement options are to crawl or to spend an amount of movement equal to half your Speed (round down) to stand up and thereby end the condition. If your Speed is 0, you can't stand up.
-  You have Disadvantage on attack rolls. An attack roll against you has Advantage if the attacker is within 5 feet of you. Otherwise, that attack roll has Disadvantage.
