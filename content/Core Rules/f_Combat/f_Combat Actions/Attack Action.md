You make an attack using a weapon you are currently wielding, or using an unarmed attack. When you make an attack, roll 1d20 and add Strength for melee or Dexterity for ranged weapons, plus your proficiency bonus. If the total is higher than the target's [[Dodge Value]], your attack hits. Roll the damage of your weapon, add STR or DEX depending on the weapon type, plus any additional bonuses you might have. The attacker takes that much damage.

For unarmed attacks, you deal damage that equals your STR modifier.

Some spells ask for an attack roll, which follow the same principles, and use the caster's spellcasting bonus instead of their STR or DEX.

Rolling a 1 on the d20 is a critical miss, regardless of your added bonuses
Rolling a 20 on the d20 is a critical hit, and instantly hits the target regardless your added bonuses. A critical hit doubles the number of rolled dice for the attack.

Sometimes, certain features allow you to make special attacks, called [[Light Attack]]s.
