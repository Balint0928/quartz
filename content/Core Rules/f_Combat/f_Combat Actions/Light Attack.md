Light attack is a less powerful version of a normal melee or ranged attack. When a feature or action calls for a Light Attack, make an attack as normal, and apply any on-hit effects as usual.

Light attacks only deal the damage dice of any attack. Meaning that you do not add your modifier to the damage roll of a Light Attack. Furthermore, you do not add any damage bonuses to light attacks, unless otherwise specified.
