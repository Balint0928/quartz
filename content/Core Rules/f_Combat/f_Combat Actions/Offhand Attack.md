You make an attack with a weapon you are wielding in your off-hand.

When you take the [[Attack Action]] on your turn, and attack with a melee weapon you are holding in one hand, you can perform a single [[Light Attack]] with a different weapon you are wielding in your other hand. If either weapon has the Thrown property, you can throw the weapon instead of making a melee attack with it. You still have to meet the [[Heft]] requirements for both weapons you are wielding.
