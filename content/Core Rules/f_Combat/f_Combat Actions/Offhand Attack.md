You make an attack with a weapon you are wielding in your off-hand.

When you take the [[Attack Action]] on your turn, and attack with a melee weapon you are holding in one hand, you can perform a single attack with a different weapon you are wielding in your other hand. You don't add your ability modifier to the damage of the offhand attack. If either weapon has the Thrown property, you can throw the weapon instead of making a melee attack with it. You still have to meet the Heft requirements for both weapons you are wielding.
