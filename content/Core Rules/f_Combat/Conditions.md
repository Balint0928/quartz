A condition is a temporary game state. The definition of a condition says how it affects its recipient, and various rules define how to end a condition. This glossary defines these conditions:

- [[Bleed]]
- [[Blinded]]
- [[Burn]]
- [[Charmed]]
- [[Deafened]]
- [[Doomed]]
- [[Dying]]
- [[Exhaustion]]
- [[Frightened]]
- [[Grappled]]
- [[Incapacitated]]
- [[Invisible]]
- [[Paralyzed]]
- [[Petrified]]
- [[Poisoned]]
- [[Prone]]
- [[Restrained]]
- [[Stunned]]
- [[Sudden Death]]
- [[Unconscious]]

By default, a condition doesn't stack with itself; a recipient either has a condition or doesn't. [[Exhaustion]] and [[Doomed]] are exceptions to that rule.
