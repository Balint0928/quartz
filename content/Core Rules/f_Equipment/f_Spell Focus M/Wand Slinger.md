draft: false
tags:

You may now dual wield wands. Whenever you you would do so, you may stack both of the wands benefits together. In addition, whenever you cast a cantrip with a single attack roll (So that means no Eldritch Blast), you may use two of that cantrip. This increases your AP by 1.
