Attacks made with the whip do not damage armor or shields (cannot be reduced by AV), however an attack with a whip can be replaced with a shove or grapple action freely using the weapons statistics. Checks made in this way add your dexterity or your strength modifier. If this check succeeds, deal this weapon’s damage.
