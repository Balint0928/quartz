### Special:
This war fan is capable of functioning two separate ways. As a bonus action, you can open one or more fans you are holding, or close them. 
- While the fan is open, it functions as a [[Scimitar]], gains the [[finesse]] and [[versatile]] traits, and deals 1d4 slashing damage. 
- While it is closed, it functions as a [[Light Hammer]], gains the [[light]] trait, and deals 1d4 bludgeoning damage. When you draw or stow the [[Tessen]], it must be in its closed form. 
- If you unlock the weapon special for this weapon, it uses a different special based on if it is in an opened or closed state. It uses the [[Scimitar]] special ([[Curved Blade]]) in an open form, and the [[Light Hammer]] special ([[Bounceback]]) in a closed form.
