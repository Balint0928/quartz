title: Bouncing Blade
draft: false
tags:

---
When you throw this weapon and hit a target, the blade bounces and hits another target within 10 feet of the original target. Roll another attack roll. If it hits, the blade bounces on with the same principles. The blade can bounce up to an amount of times that equals your proficiency bonus. Each target after the first only receives the base damage die of the weapon as damage, without any bonuses.
