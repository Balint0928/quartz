---
title: Lock the Blade
draft: false
tags:
  - weapon_mastery
---
When you successfully hit with an attack using this weapon, and the target is wielding bladed weapon with a [[Heft]] of 4 or lower, instead of dealing damage, you can choose to lock down that blade. A creature can only make an attack with a locked blade, if on its turn it succeeds on a grapple check against you or uses one of its attacks to free the weapon.
