You can open or close the fan as a free action after or before you make an attack with them. Additionally, the first time you attack a creature in a combat with your open fan, they count as being unaware against that attack. If you hit an unaware enemy with your [[Tessen]], you can choose to deal your entire weapon damage as [[Bleed]].
