An enchanted item, is a special variant of a mundane item, that has special bonuses/ benefits. Enchanted items have at least one "[[Affix]]", a special modifier. The higher the item's rarity, the more affixes it can have, and the better those affixes are. The rarities are as follows:

- **Uncommon** - One low-tier affix
- **Rare** - Two low-tier affixes
- **Epic** - One low-tier and one high-tier affix
- **Legendary** - One legendary affix, at least two affixes of low or high-tier

A character can only benefit from an enchanted item, if:
- The character is proficient with the use of the item
- The item is identified
- The item is currently equipped in one of the character's [[Equipment Slots]]
