Affixes are the special properties on Enchanted items. Affixes can be Prefixes or Suffixes, based on where they are added to the item's name. A Prefix is always added before the item's name, and the Suffix is added after. By default, an item cannot have two Pre or Suffixes, unless it is a Legendary rarity item. Additionally, an item can only have one of the same kind of affix at once.

Certain Affixes have values in square brackets in their descriptions. These are the maximum and minimum of that value, and the actual value is determined by the DM, when the item is discovered/identified.
