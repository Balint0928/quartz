While wielding a weapon with a default [[Heft]] of at least 6, you gain the following benefits:

- When you attack an enemy and deal damage to them, as a bonus action, you may select an ally and allow them to move up to their base walking speed. This doesn't provoke [[Opportunity Attack]]s.
- When you miss with an attack on your turn, you can use your bonus action to allow an ally to make one attack against the same creature.
