While wielding a [[Net]] and a weapon in the other hand, you gain the following benefits:

- Whenever you would attack a [[Restrained]] enemy, you treat your [[Heft]] benefits as 3 higher. 
- Whenever your [[Net]] is destroyed, you can make an attack as a free action with the weapon in your other hand if you are within range.
