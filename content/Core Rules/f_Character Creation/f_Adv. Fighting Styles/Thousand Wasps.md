While wielding darts, you gain the following benefits:

- When you throw a [[Dart]], you may throw an additional [[Dart]]. This feature doesn't trigger itself or features like [[Scatter throw]] or [[Improved Scatter]].
- All damage done by your [[Dart]]s and their properties are added together before [[Armor Value]] or other [[Damage Mitigation]] is applied.
