*Prerequisite: [[Dwarf]] Ancestry, [[The Ixalanti|Ixalanti]] Region*

When you use the [[Guard Up]] special or mastery, you can make a melee [[Light Attack]] as a free action against an adjacent enemy.

When a creature that is larger than you, hits you with an attack, reduce the damage from that attack by an amount that equals the amount of steps the creature is larger than you. If the creature is a Monstrosity, double this bonus, if it is an insectoid, triple it.
