A special type of [[ammunition]] usable with [[Crossbow, Heavy]] or [[Crossbow, light]].

While you are adjacent to an enemy you may fire a shrapnel bolt. Shrapnel Bolt is a 15 foot cone, you roll to attack every creature within this cone as if targeted by a ranged [[Light Attack]]. This item may be crafted by using one charge from a [[Smithing Tools]] over the course of 1 hour, each Shrapnel Bolt has a weight of 2 bulk.
