---
While wielding a [[Longsword]] in one hand, nothing else in the other hand and not wearing Heavy Armor, you gain the following benefits:

- Whenever you hit an enemy with your [[Longsword]], you gain a bonus to your [[Dodge Value]] that equals the [[Heft]] of your [[Longsword]]. This doesn't stack with other [[Dodge Value]] increasing effects or itself.
- Whenever an enemy misses you with a melee attack, all adjacent enemies take the weapon damage of your [[Longsword]]. This can happen twice per round.
