While wielding a [[Light Shield]] in one hand, and a [[Trident]] in the other, you gain the following benefits:

- When you throw your Trident, you can return it to your hand as a free action. (This can be because of a mystical attunement, or a length of chain or rope attached to the weapon)
- [[Hindering Blow]] lasts for as long as the target has [[Bleed]].
