*Prerequisite: [[Elf]] Ancestry*

While wielding a [[Quarterstaff]] with two hands, whenever you would deal damage to an enemy, you increase the Heft of the weapons they wield by 1, this lasts for 1 round. (This does not stack) If this causes the Heft to be too great to wield the enemy drops said weapon.
