---
While wielding a weapon with a base [[Heft]] of 8, you gain the following benefits:

- An enemy hit by your weapon, that is benefiting from a concentration spell, or a spell that provides [[Armor Value]], [[Dodge Value]] or Temporary hit points, the controller of that spell must make a DC 15 concentration check or the spell is removed from the target. This doesn't count as losing concentration for the caster.
