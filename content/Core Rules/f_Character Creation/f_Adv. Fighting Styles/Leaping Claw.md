While wielding a [[Sickle]] or a [[Khopesh]] gain the following benefits:

- You may make a [[Light Attack]] with your [[Sickle]] or [[Khopesh]] as a free action at the end of your movement. This triggers once for each [[Sickle]] or [[Khopesh]] you are wielding. Once triggered, you reduce your movement to 0 until the start of your next turn.
- If you take the [[Dash Action]], the attacks are made from advantage.
