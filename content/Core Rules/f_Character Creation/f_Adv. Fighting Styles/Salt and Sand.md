While wielding a [[Crossbow, Hand|Hand Crossbow]] and a melee weapon in the other hand, you gain the following benefits:

- You can fire your [[Crossbow, Hand|Hand Crossbow]]  in melee without any penalties
- Whenever you make an [[Offhand Attack]] with your melee weapon, you may fire your [[Crossbow, Hand|Hand Crossbow]] and add its damage to the damage roll of your other weapon.
