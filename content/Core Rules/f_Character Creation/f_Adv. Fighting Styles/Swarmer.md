*Prerequisite: [[Eskhal]] Ancestry, [[Martial]] Role*

While wielding your [[Insectoid Weapon]], you gain the following benefits:

- Whenever you attack an enemy, if you hit that target the next ally that attacks that target has advantage on their next attack roll.
- Whenever you would critically hit a target, your attack deals an extra damage die worth of damage against that target.
