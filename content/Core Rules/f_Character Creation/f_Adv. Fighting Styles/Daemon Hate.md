While wielding a [[Greatsword]], you gain the following benefits:

- You may add half the [[Heft]] of your weapon to your [[Armor Value]], whenever you are attacked by weapons with a [[Heft]] of 4 or lower. 
- If the attacker is a Fiend or a Daemon, you add your level to your [[Armor Value]] against that attack.
