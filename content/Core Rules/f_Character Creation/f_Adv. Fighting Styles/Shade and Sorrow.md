draft: false
tags:
---
While wielding a [[Club]] and [[Dagger]], you gain the following benefits:

- Whenever you critically hit with a [[Club]], the next attack with your [[Dagger]] you make before the start of your next turn is also a critical hit if it hits.
- You consider the positive benefits of [[Heft]] of your [[Club]] and [[Dagger]] to be half of the target's current [[Bleed]].
