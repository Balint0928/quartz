*Prerequisite: [[Halfling]] Ancestry*

While wielding a [[Shortsword]] and nothing else in the other hand, you deal all of your damage as [[Bleed]] damage. In addition, whenever an enemy you can see removes their [[Bleed]] using an action, and you have damaged them with your [[Shortsword]] this combat, they take half of their [[Bleed]] damage immediately.
