---
title: Shifting Tides
draft: false
tags:
---
While wielding a [[Buckler]] in one hand and a weapon in the other, you gain the following benefits:

- Whenever an enemy misses you with an attack, you may move 5ft. If this moves you out of the range of an enemy, you may make a [[Light Attack]] against them as a free action with the weapon in your other hand.
- If you use the [[Riposte]] or [[Improved Riposte]] feature with the [[Buckler]] and your attack deals damage to the target, you may instead move the enemy 5ft within or out of your reach.
