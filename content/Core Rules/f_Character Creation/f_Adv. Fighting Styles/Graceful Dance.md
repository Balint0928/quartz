tags:
---
When wielding a weapon with the [[dueling]] trait with one hand and nothing in the other hand you are able to use your natural grace to dance in combat, pressing the advantage.

Whenever an enemy would take damage from a bleed effect, you may move five feet. this does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

Whenever you move outside of your turn, you deal +1 damage to the next melee damage roll you make.
