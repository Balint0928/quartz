*Prerequisite: [[Martial]] role, [[Dwarf]] Ancestry*

Your [[Ancestral Training]] role passive can now trigger a number of times that equals your proficiency bonus, per [[Long Rest]].

In addition, while using a [[Greataxe]], [[Improved Cleave]] grants you a normal attack, instead of a [[Light Attack]].
