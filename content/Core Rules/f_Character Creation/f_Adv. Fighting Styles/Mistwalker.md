*Prerequisite: [[The Misted Lands]] region, [[Elf]] Ancestry*

While you are in a lightly or heavily obscured space, other creatures cannot gain advantage on attacks against you because they are hidden. 

Additionally, while you are in a cloud of mist, dust, fog or similar effect, your movement speed increases by 10 feet, and your first weapon attack each turn has its critical threshold increased by 1.
