*Prerequisite: [[Orc]] Ancestry*

 Once per long rest, when you take damage of any kind, you can gain resistance against that damage type that equals your Constitution modifier. The next time you are hit with a different damage type, you gain Weakness against that damage type that equals your Constitution modifier. This lasts until you finish a long rest. This Resistance and Weakness can stack with other sources.
