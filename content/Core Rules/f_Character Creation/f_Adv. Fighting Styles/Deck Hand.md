While wielding a non-Cestus weapon in your main hand, and a [[Cestus]] in the other, gain the following benefits:

- If you attack and miss with your non-Cestus weapon, you can make an [[Light Attack]] with your [[Cestus]] as a free action. If this attack hits, you have advantage on your next attack with your non-Cestus weapon.
- If you critically hit with your [[Cestus]], your non-Cestus weapon has its critical threshold increased on its next attack that equals half your [[Vigor]]. This doesn't stack with other critical manipulation effects.
