While wielding a [[Sling]], you gain the following benefits:

- Your attacks with a [[Sling]] have the [[Impale]] feature. 
- If you critically hit with your [[Sling]], the projectile bounces and attacks other creatures within 15 feet of the original target. These attacks occur until you miss once. These attacks cannot be applied to a creature more than once, and this feature doesn't trigger [[Shrapnel shot]].
