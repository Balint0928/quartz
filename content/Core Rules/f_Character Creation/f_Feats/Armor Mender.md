---
title: Armor Mender
draft: false
tags:
  - feat
---
You gain the ability to Mend the armor of you and your companions, even when you have no good tools or resources on hand. You can spend your short rest, restoring 1 AV to the armor of up to an amount of creatures that equals your proficiency bonus. This can happen twice per long rest.

If you have proficiency with the [[Smithing Tools]], each charge you expend, restores an additional AV to the armor or shield.
