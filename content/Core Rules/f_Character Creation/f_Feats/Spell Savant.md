---
title: Spell Savant
draft: false
tags:
  - feat
---
Choose a single spell (not cantrip) you know how to cast and can cast with spell slots. When you cast that spell, you increase its damage die by one step, you increase its DC by 3 and you may cast that spell even if you could not normally. (Note you must be conscious)

This has to be an actual spell, and a spell modified by the [[Wizard]]'s [[Arcane Grimoire]] cannot be chosen.
