title: Combat Medic
draft: false
tags:

You gain proficiency with the Healer’s Kit.

If you already have proficiency, you gain [[Tool Mastery]] with it.

In [[combat]], when you take the Dodge action, you can expend a charge and use your healer’s kit to restore hit points to a creature within 5 feet of you. The target creature also gains the benefit of your Dodge action, until the start of their next turn.

You can use your healer’s kit this way even if you have a weapon, [[light shield]] or [[buckler]] wielded in your hands.
