The permanent AP granted by [[Font of Aether]] increases from 2 to 5.

You are immune to gaining AP from spells that you don't cast by expanding a spell slot (ex: magic items, scrolls etc)

Whenever you damage a creature in combat with a cantrip you cast, you reduce your current AP by 1. This can only happen proficiency bonus number of times. This refreshes on a long rest.
