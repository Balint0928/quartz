draft: false
tags:

---
Increase your critical hit threshold by 1. In addition if you score a critical hit on a creature who is not on its native plane of existence or other summoned creature, that creature is subject to an effect like the Banishment spell, this follows the same effect as the spell. If the creature passes the save, it instead takes additional force damage equal to your character level.
