_Prerequisite: [[Deity]] with at least 3L divine focus_

When you use your [[Hymn]] ability, you and each creature within 30 feet must roll a 1d20. On an even roll, a creature can reduce their current [[Exhaustion]] or AP by 1. On an odd roll, a creature must expend a Hit die or gain a point of [[Exhaustion]]. A creature can only benefit from this sermon once per [[long rest]].
