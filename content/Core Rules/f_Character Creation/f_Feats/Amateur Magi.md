_Prerequisite: Character does not have the [[Magical]] role_

Learn two cantrips from any spell list (except for special cantrips granted by class features, like Eldritch Blast) and a 1st level spell. You can cast that spell once per [[long rest]]. The spellcasting ability for the spell is chosen when you take this feat (INT, WIS, CHA)
