Whenever you would gain AP, reduce the amount gained by 1. You can do so a number of times that equals your character level per long rest.

If you should reach 10 AP with your [[Aether Fatigue]] feature, an Aether Event does not happen, instead, you enter Apotheosis for 3 rounds. (If you reach 10 AP on your turn, and you still have your action available, you enter Apotheosis on that turn. Otherwise, you enter it at the start of your next turn.)

At the end of your Apotheosis, your AP is set to 9, and you cannot enter apotheosis again until you have finished an [[Extended Rest]] and your AP is reset to your minimum.

Apotheosis grants you different effects based on your roles.

## Apotheosis
- [[Magical]] role: Your magic acts as a beacon for an otherworldly force. A supernatural entity lends you its power. If you are a [[Warlock]] or [[Cleric]], this is probably your [[Patron]] or [[Deity]]. The effect of this assistance is ambiguous and depends on the entity and the situation. (It might be lending you extra spells, summon a creature to assist you or replicate a spell effect)
