- Gain a +1 bonus to an ability score of your choice

- When you finish a [[Long Rest]], you can choose one of the following features to be active until the end of your next [[Long Rest]]:

**Charioteer:** Gain proficiency in the Chariot and gain the ability to maneuver it and use it in combat.

**Carriage Guard:** You can drive and steer a carriage without using up your hand slots. In addition, you cannot be off-balanced or dismounted while on a carriage.

**Privateer:** Whenever you would use a skill check to board an enemy vehicle, you have advantage on those skill checks. Whenever you attempt to sabotage an enemy vehicle, you have advantage on rolls to do so and deal double damage to vehicles.
