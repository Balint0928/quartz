Whenever you would use one of your respective [[Monk Tradition]] features, you also gain the following benefit:

- Mantra of the Sun’s Kiss: You can use the base ability as a bonus action if you spend 2 [[spirit]] instead of 1.
- Mantra of the Moon’s Veil: While this mantra is active, you can spend 2 [[spirit]] to use a bonus action to teleport to a space you can see and isn’t in bright light.
- Way of the Warden: Whenever you would make an attack with a monk weapon you can increase the critical threshold of that weapon by 2 for 1 round if you expend 2 [[spirit]]. (This does not function with unarmed strikes.)
