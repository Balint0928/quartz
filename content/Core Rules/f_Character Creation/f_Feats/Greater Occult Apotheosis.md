*Prerequisite: [[Lesser Occult Apotheosis]] feat.*

- The AP reduction from [[Lesser Occult Apotheosis]], increases by 1.

- Your Apotheosis lasts for 1 minute instead of 3 rounds.

- When you would reach 15 AP, you gain a special Occult Aetheric Form, chosen when you first enter this state.
