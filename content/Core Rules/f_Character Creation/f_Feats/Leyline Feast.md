The permanent AP granted by [[Font of Aether]] increases from 2 to 5.

Whenever you use [[Open the Wellspring]], regain an extra spell slot. Additionally, you and allies gain 1 AP for you and each ally who is also using [[Aether Channel]]. At the end of the [[Short Rest]], you create a pool of healing points that equals the amount of AP gained by all the participants of this effect. Distribute that pool as HP amongst these creatures.
