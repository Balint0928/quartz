You gain the ability to create blessed scriptures, written blessings and litanies from your [[Deity]]’s worship. You can create one of these scriptures during a 1 hour séance and 100 GP. You can have a maximum amount of these scriptures that equals your proficiency bonus. A creature wearing a scripture gain a +1 bonus on a saving throw of your choice (chosen when you create the scripture). When a creature wearing a scripture of yours rolls a death saving throw, you can use your reaction to burn the scripture (destroying it) and restore 1 HP to the target. A character can only benefit from this effect once per [[Long Rest]].
