You gain the ability to craft weapons, and apply [[Affix]] to them.
- You can create low and high tier affixes of this type up to a level equal to half your character level (rounded down). Workshops and other modifiers might affect this.
- You can gather recipes while you have this feat and learn them similarly to how a spell is learned.
- You may also experiment to discover new recipes. This consumes resources regardless of success.
