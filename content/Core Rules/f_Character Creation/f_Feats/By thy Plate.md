This feat is active when:
- Whenever you are at half of your hit points or lower.
- Your [[Aetherial Arms]] is applied to your armor.

While this feat is active, your armor can regenerate 1 point of AV each turn as a bonus action.

Once per [[short rest]] while the above passive is active, as a bonus action you may focus all of your Aether into your armor. If you do, for the next 3 rounds, damage rolls from weapons deal minimum damage against you.
