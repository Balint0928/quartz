The permanent AP granted by [[Font of Aether]] increases from 3 to 5.

The extra damage feature from [[Font of Aether]] now also applies to all creatures within 20 feet of you whenever you deal damage with a spell. If a creature would die from this effect, this effect triggers again. This continues until it stops triggering.
