When you use your [[Lay of the Land]] feature, you can also choose one of the following benefits, that matches the environment:

- You can speak, read, and write two additional languages of your choice.
- Gain Swimming speed that equals your walking speed
- Gain Climbing speed that equals your walking speed
- Gain Low-Light vision in a 30 foot radius. If you already have low-light vision, gain Dark Vision instead
