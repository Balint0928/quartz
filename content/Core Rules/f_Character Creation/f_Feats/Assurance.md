- Increase an ability score of your choice by 1

- Gain proficiency or [[Skill Mastery]] with a skill of your choice.

- Choose a skill you are proficient in. You can, when rolling a skill check with that skill, forgo rolling and instead gain a result of 10 + your proficiency bonus. No bonuses apply to this except for expertise.
