*Prerequisite: Pact of the Blade*

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can expend a [[warlock]] spell slot to deal an extra 1d6 force damage to the target, plus another 1d6 per level of the spell slot. For each level of spell slots expended this way, gain 1 AP.
