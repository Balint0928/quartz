You gain proficiency with [[Poisoner's Supplies]] and [[Alchemist Supplies]]. If you already have proficiency with these kits, you gain [[Tool Mastery]] instead.

You can apply two doses of poison or oil to a single weapon or stack of ammunition at the same time.

You can also apply these as a bonus action to the weapon and ammunition stack.
