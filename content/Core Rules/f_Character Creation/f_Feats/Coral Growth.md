title: Coral Growth
draft: false
tags:
  - feat
---
- Gain the ability to summon a Coral Growth, once per [[Short Rest]]. When you summon the Coral, choose one:
	- You summon the Coral as an action, in an unoccupied space within 20 feet. The Coral occupies a 5 foot cube, and provides full cover. It has 20 HP and an AV of 5. The coral remains for a minute.
	- You summon the Coral as an action targeting a medium or smaller hostile creature within 20 feet. The target must succeed a strength saving throw (DC=8+prof+CON) or become [[Restrained]] by the coral until the end of your next turn.
	- You summon the Coral as a bonus action, targeting an ally or willing creature within 20 feet. The creature is encased in the Coral until the end of your next turn. While in the coral, they are behind full cover, [[Incapacitated]], and can only use their action to spend and roll Hit Dice to recover HP. When they do so, they can add your Constitution modifier to the roll as well.
