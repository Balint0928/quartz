This feat is active when:
- Whenever you are at half of your hit points or lower.
- Your [[Aetherial Arms]] is applied to your shield.

While this feat is active, when you receive healing, you also gain temporary HP that equals your CHA bonus.

Once per short rest, while the above passive is active, as a bonus action you may focus your Aether into your shield. When you do so you may project a spectral image of your shield around yourself or an ally within 30 feet of you for 3 rounds. While the shield is projected this way, the target is considered protected by it, without needing to use the [[Guard]] bonus action, and if the shield loses AV this way, it won't reduce your original shield's AV.
