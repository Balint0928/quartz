You gain the ability to forge a link with a powerful, Aether-rich location, referred to as a Touchstone site.

Touchstone sites are scattered across the world and come in many shapes and sizes. With this feat, you gain the ability to attune yourself to one such location, and gain a special benefit connected to the Touchstone.

In order to attune to a Touchstone site you must fulfill one of the following prerequisites:
- Thorough and familiar knowledge of the Touchstone site.
- Possession of a *Touchstone Key* (a portable object native to the touchstone's area and worth at least 250 GP)
For the attunement process, you have to complete a day long meditation and a ritual. The rare incense and materials for the ritual cost 250 GP which the ritual consumes. Alternatively you can use the Touchstone Key in place of the material component for the ritual (the key is also consumed by the ritual).

Once you are attuned to a Touchstone Site, you gain its basic Touchstone Ability. If you physically visit the location to which you are attuned, you can also gain a higher-order ability with a limited number of uses. You can swap sites any time you visit a new one and fulfill its higher-order recharge condition.
