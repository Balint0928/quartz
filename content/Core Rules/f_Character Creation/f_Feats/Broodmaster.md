tags:

- You can cast the spell [[Infestation]] at will. If you don't have the [[Spellcasting]] feature, you use your Constitution for the Save DC.

- When you cast this spell, it is not considered as spellcasting. 

- When you cast this spell as an action, the damage increases to 1d8 and you can target up to an amount of targets that equals your proficiency bonus.

- When you are hit with an attack, and the attacker is within 15 feet of you, you can cast [[Infestation]] as a reaction, targeting that creature.
