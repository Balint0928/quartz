This feat is active when:
- Whenever you are at half of your hit points or lower.
- Your [[Aetherial Arms]] is applied to your melee weapon.

While this is active, gain a 1d6 bonus damage that matches the damage type of your [[Aetherial Arms]], to your melee weapon attacks.

Once per short rest, while the above passive is active, as a bonus action you may focus your Aether into your weapon, if you do, for the next 3 rounds, all extra damage from this feature is maximized.
