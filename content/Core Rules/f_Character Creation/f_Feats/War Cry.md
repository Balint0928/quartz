title: War Cry
draft: false
tags:
  - feat
---
Whenever you would roll initiative and you are not afflicted with the Surprised condition. You may scream at the top of your lungs and make an Intimidation check against all enemies that can hear you. All enemies must make an opposing Insight or Intimidation check against your roll. If they fail they gain the Frightened condition for a number of rounds equal to your proficiency modifier.

You deal your proficiency modifier in extra damage against targets with the Frightened condition.
