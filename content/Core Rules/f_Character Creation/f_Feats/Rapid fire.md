*Prerequisite: Pact of the Cannon*

You gain an extra feature you can choose for your Pact of the Cannon feature:
- **Rapid Fire:**
	As a bonus action, you temporarily duplicate your cannon to unleash a flurry of eldritch energy. All creatures within a 15-foot cone in front of you must succeed a Dexterity saving throw against your spell save DC or take 2d10 damage of the same type as your Eldritch Blast. To use this bonus action, you need to be holding your cannon in one hand and have the other hand free. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
