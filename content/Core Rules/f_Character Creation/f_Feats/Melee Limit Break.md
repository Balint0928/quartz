# Limit Gauge
If at least one character has a *Limit Break* feat in a party, the party unlocks the Limit Gauge. The Gauge has a maximum charges that equals **Number of PCs x Party LvL x 10** (Minimum 100). The Gauge is filled when a PC takes damage in a combat encounter (the dmg doesn't have to reduce the HP). When that happens, add the damage taken to the Gauge. The following restrictions apply to the Limit Gauge:

- Damage is only added once per effect. If an effect would deal damage multiple times or overtime, only add the first instance.
- AOE or multi target damage is only added once, regardless of the amount of PCs effected by the damage.
- Damage is only added in combat.
- The Gauge starts each combat from 0 and empties out at the end of it.
- The Gauge is a shared pool, all characters contribute to it, but only characters with Limit Break features can use it.

# Melee Limit Break
Once per [[Long Rest]], you can activate this LB as an action, it costs 100 charges, and it grants you benefits based on your roles. As part of activating this LB, make a melee attack from advantage against a creature in range.

- [[Martial]] role: If the attack hits, it is a guaranteed critical hit.
- [[Mundane]] role: You add your total character level to the damage and attack roll. If this attack would miss, the target still takes 2x your character level damage. (Type same as your weapon)
- [[Wanderer]] role: This attack and all of your melee attacks for the next 3 rounds can deal magical damage instead of physical (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Thunder, Poison, Acid,). If this would trigger a weakness, double the damage of the weakness.
