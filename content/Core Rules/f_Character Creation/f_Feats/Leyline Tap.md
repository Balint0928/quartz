Whenever you use [[Aether Channel]], you may instead activate this ability. (This feature cannot trigger class features that enhance or modify [[Aether Channel]])
- Gain 4 AP.
- Recover all of your expended spell slots.
- 2 of the 4 AP gained is permanent until the end of your next [[Extended Rest]].

You cannot use this feature again, until you finish an [[Extended Rest]] that is at least 48 hours long.
