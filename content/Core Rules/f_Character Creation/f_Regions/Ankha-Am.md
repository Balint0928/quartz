# Lore
The once verdant wetlands of Ankha now lay demolished, looted and depleted. The Sun Sultan's conquest made the peoples of the region broken, and cowering in fear, the land stipped of its resources.
The ancient people of Ankhera also benefited from the life giving waters of the rivers and wetlands of Ankha-Am, that flows into the sea. Nowadays, the river is drying up, and carries diseases and poison in its waters.

The Ankhans revered the following old gods:
- Ankha - Ancient spirit of life, sacrificed herself to give life to the people of the desert
- Anubis - Ancient spirit of death and passing, many believe that it is his curse that afflicts the sleepless sands. Not widely worshipped anymore. Sacred animal is a Jackal.
- Anxarch - "The Wings that carry us", ancient spirit of the skies and flight. Sacred animal is an Eagle
- Amutokh - "The Eyes that observe", ancient spirit of wisdom, insight and knowledge. Sacred animal is a peacock.
- Renenet - "The darkness that contrasts our light" - ancient spirit of twilight, hunts and warriors. Sacred animal is a Cat.
- Quat - "Reminds us of the past, and guides us to the future", ancient spirit of guidance and foresight. Sacred animal is a Ram.

# Major Settlement(s)
Ankhera was the capital city of a once great and rich kingdom. Now, amidst its ruined patina, the broken people of Ankhara live under the control of the Sun Sultan's soldiers. The leadership of the city is divided between bickering church officials of the old gods of Ankha, who never see eye-to-eye enough to unite their people against the tyrant.
