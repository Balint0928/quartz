# Lore
The lush forests and damp jungles of the north, called the Ixalanti, is home to the most dangerous and largest flora and fauna of all of Iqor. People that call this place their home are either foolish enough to stay, or strong enough to survive. The jungles are littered with ancient sites and temples of an ancient lost civilization, that disappeared long ago.
