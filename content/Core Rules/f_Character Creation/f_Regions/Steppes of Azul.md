# Lore
The other largest region in all of Iqor. Azul is quite the opposite of the Sleepless Sands. Steppes, rolling hills, forests and meadows. The land is the home of the Azlanti Tribes, a people of nomadic tribes who wander all over Azul. Many who flee the terrors of the sands find refuge with one of the many tribes.

# Major Settlement(s)
The only important permanent settlement in this region is Azul-Xan the gathering place of the Azuli tribes.
