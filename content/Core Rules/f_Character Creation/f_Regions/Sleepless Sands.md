# Lore
The largest region in all of Iqor, the sleepless sands encompass many other regions, cultures and peoples. Civilization mainly centers around two great rivers and wetlands, the Najat and the Ankha-Am. 

The undisputed ruler of the sands is the Sun Sultan. Ruling from the majestic city of Sadirah, the sultan is a tyrant feared by many. 

The Sleepless Sands is under an ancient and powerful curse, that makes the nights extremely dangerous. When the sun sets over the dunes, the dark of the night summons powerful daemons and ravenous dead to terrorize the living. Drawn by the light and the warmth of life, these creatures keep the folks of the sands cowering by candles and behind closed doors.

However, the constant exposure to such horrors, made the people of the sands resilient, as they stand up and fight to rid their homeland from these nightly terrors.

# Major Settlement(s)
The largest, safest, and richest settlement in all of Iqor is Sadirah, the city where the sun never sets. The imposing towers, promenades, market districts and noble estates make this city a sight to behold. Yet none of these command attention as much as the miniature blazing sun floating above the royal palace all the time. The sun, or the Eye of the Sultan, provides light and protection from the creatures of the night. Living within the city walls, and close to the eye is a privilege the sultan gives and takes at his leisure.
