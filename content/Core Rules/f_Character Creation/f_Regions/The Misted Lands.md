# Lore
A warm, and mist covered land, for the longest time, home to the majority of Elves on Iqor. A few decades ago, a conquering nation from another continent to the south arrived at the shores, and finding a similar climate to their homeland, settled down. After a still ongoing bloody war with the indigenous elves, the misted lands are far from the peaceful paradise it appears to be. 

The Misted Lands are saturated with ambient Aether, enhancing anyone who were born there.

# Major Settlement(s)
Nakashiyako is the capital of the invading kingdom on Iqor. Its pagodas and temples rise out of the thick forests and wetlands of the surrounding area.
