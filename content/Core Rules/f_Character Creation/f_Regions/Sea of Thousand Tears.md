# Lore
A Mediterranean sea flanked by mountains, and the deserts to the south. The sea itself is home to many [[Itzcali]], but the hundreds of islands, and port cities around the sea also teems with life. Free from the yoke of the Sun Sultan, people of the Tears indulge themselves in trading and maritime activities. 

# Major Settlement(s)
Akiya stands on a peak overlooking the wide strait that connects the sea to the ocean. Akiya is a city of commerce, where everything has a price.
