## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: You must be a level 5 [[Fighter]] or [[Rogue]]*

- Hit dice: [[Fighter]] gains 1d8, [[Rogue]] gains 1d10

- [[Rogue]] gains: [[Second Wind]], [[Warrior’s Soul]]

**Level 7**
- [[Fighter]] gains: [[Cunning Action]]
- [[Rogue]] gains: [[Action Surge]]

**Level 8**
- [[Insult to Injury]]

**Level 9**
- [[Blurring Hands]]

**Level 10**
- [[Nice Try]]
