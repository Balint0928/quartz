## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: You must be a level 5 [[Fighter]] or [[Wizard]]*

- Hit dice: [[Fighter]] gets 1d6, [[Wizard]] gets 1d10

# Features by level
**Level 6**
- [[Tome of War]]
- [[Mana of War]]
- [[Fighter]] gets: [[Spellcasting]], [[Arcane Grimoire]], Arcana proficiency
- [[Wizard]] gets: [[Second Wind]], [[Warrior’s Soul]]

**Level 7**
- [[Fighter]] gets: [[Chant]]
- [[Wizard]] gets: [[Action Surge]]

**Level 8**
- [[Wave of War]]

**Level 9**
- [[Pillar of Prowess]]

**Level 10**
- [[Storm of Spell and Steel]]
