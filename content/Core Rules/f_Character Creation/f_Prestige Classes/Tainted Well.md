## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: Must be a 5th level [[Sorcerer]] or [[Warlock]].*

- Hit dice: [[Sorcerer]] gains 1d8, [[Warlock]] gains 1d8

# Features by level
**Level 6**
- [[Deep Reserves]]
- [[Tainted Aether]]
- [[Sorcerer]] gains: [[Spellcasting Progression]], [[Heretic's Hunger]] - Sorcerers are now only able to regain 1 spell slot when using [[Aether Channel]].
- [[Warlock]] gains: [[Spellcasting Progression]], [[Font of Aether]]

**Level 7**
- [[Sorcerer]] gains: [[Eldritch Invocation]]
- [[Warlock]] gains: [[Open the Wellspring]]

**Level 8**
- [[Cursed Hunger]]

**Level 9**
- [[Rupture Aether]]

**Level 10**
- [[Quietus]]
