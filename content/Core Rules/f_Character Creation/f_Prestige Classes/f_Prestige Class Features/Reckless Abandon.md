---
title: Reckless Abandon
draft: false
tags:
  - class_feature
---
Whenever you make a spell attack roll, you can choose spend a point of Divine Focus to benefit from the Recklessness emotion of your [[Trance]], even if you are not in a [[Trance]]. If you do so, all allies within 30 feet gain advantage on their next attack roll they make. If the attack you make would kill or critically hit an enemy, all allies within 30 ft are healed equal to the amount your [[Blood of the Fallen]] would heal you.
