draft: false
tags:

You may perform a dance that infuses an ally within 30ft with increased power for their Spells. The next spell the affected ally casts, is automatically cast at their highest spell slot but it costs them the normal spell slot for that Spell. 

You may use this ability a number of times per long rest equal to your Wisdom or Charisma modifier, whichever is higher.
