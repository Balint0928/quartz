You lose your bard songs from [[Maestro]], and instead gain the use of Ancient Dances. You also gain an additional use of these dances. You may only have one such dance active at a time. However, you may change the dance that is active whenever you use [[Dance of Spirits]]. Changing the dance counts as activating it.

- **Dance of Thorny Vine:** All allies within 30ft gain +3 to all of their damage rolls, until their next rest.
- **Dance of Soothing Winds:** All Allies within 30ft are healed for an additional 3 hit points whenever they are healed, until their next rest.
- **Dance of Lonely Glens:** All allies within 30ft gain a +1 to DV for each ally PC they are not adjacent to, until their next rest.
- **Dance of Shillelagh:** All allies within 30ft gain a +1 to attack rolls for each ally they are adjacent to, until their next rest.
