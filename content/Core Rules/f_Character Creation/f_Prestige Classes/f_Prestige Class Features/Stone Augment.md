Whenever you would spend a spell slot to cast a spell, you may augment that spell with your stones to deal additional damage. If you do, you expend 1 stone per application of damage. The extra damage is 1d6.
(Magic missile uses 1 and applies damage once to a single missile, scorching ray uses three and applies the damage to each ray, fireball uses 1 and applies the damage once to all enemies affected.)
