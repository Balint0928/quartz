title: Sneaky Chant
draft: false
tags:

---
Whenever you use your [[Chant]] ability the next spell you cast adds your [[Sneak Attack]] damage to the total spell damage done. 

In addition, whenever you would refresh three spells with [[Spell Shave]] in a combat, you refresh your [[Chant]] ability as well. This can occur once in a combat.
