Once per [[Long Rest]], you can use an action to convert up to half of your unspent Coins from your [[Personal Mint]] feature, back into GP. When you do so, you create a pool of HP that equals twice the regained GP. You can immediately restore HP to any creatures or yourself within 30 feet of you, by spending points from this pool.
