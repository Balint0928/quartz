You can become a whirling dancer of death as a free action at the start of your turn. While in this dance, you gain the following benefits:

- Your walking movement speed increases by 5 feet.
- You can take the [[Dash Action]] as a bonus action.
- When you take the attack action, and move at least 5 feet before each attack, you can add your proficiency bonus to the damage roll of that attack, even if the attack is a [[Light Attack]].
- During your turn, when you make a melee attack against a creature, that creature can't make [[Opportunity Attack]]s against you for the rest of the turn.
 
While under the effects of the dance, you have the following restrictions:

- You cannot move into a space you already occupied in this turn, and you cannot end your turn in the same space where you started it. 
- If you critically miss an attack or would be made [[Prone]] or immobile in any way, your dance ends and you lose all benefits. 
- You can end the Dance as a free action.
- The dance also ends if you don't move at least 5 feet right before each attack on your turn or you don't make any attacks on your turn.
- You may only perform this dance wielding slashing weapons. 
- You cannot perform this dance while wearing heavy armor or while wielding a shield. 
- While dancing, you cannot use actions, features and abilities that requires you to stay still such as hiding. 
- You may enter this dance a number of times per long rest that equals your proficiency bonus.
