During a [[Long Rest]], you can transform GP into special Coins. You can have a maximum amount of Coins that equals 5 x your charisma modifier. These Coins are special, and have no trade value. They can be used however, to fuel your special abilities unlike normal GP. When an ability uses your Coins, the coins are consumed and cannot be used again.

Features using your Coins will use your CHA modifier for attack and damage rolls as well as save DCs.
