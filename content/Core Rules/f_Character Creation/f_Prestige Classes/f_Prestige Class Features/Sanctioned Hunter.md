When you use your [[Hunter's Mark]] on a creature, you can expend a Divine Focus to gain extra benefits based on the type of Focus expended:

- Law - If the marked creature would deal non-physical damage to you, you reduce the damage taken by your [[Armor Value]].
- Chaos - Your attacks against the marked creature bypasses resistances, and ignores natural AV of the target by an amount that equals your proficiency modifier.
