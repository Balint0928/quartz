You may use your [[Aether Channel]] feature as an action. You may use and benefit from this ability a number of times equal to your spellcasting ability modifier per [[Extended Rest]], and you cannot use its AP reduction feature. If you do, you gain 1 AP. Additionally, enemies within 30 ft. must make a spellcasting ability saving throw (or Constitution) against DC 10 + your total character level. If they fail, you may increase their current AP by 2.
