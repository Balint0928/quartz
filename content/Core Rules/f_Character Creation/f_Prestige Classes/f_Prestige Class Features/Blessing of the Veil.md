draft: false
tags:

---
As an Action, select an ally and spend a point of Divine Focus, that ally has any spell it is benefiting from to be infused with increased power, that ally increases one incremental benefit of that spell by 1. (a +1 becomes a +2, 1d4 becomes 2d4, 30ft becomes 60ft) (GM discretion). This effect lasts for a number of rounds equal to your proficiency modifier.
