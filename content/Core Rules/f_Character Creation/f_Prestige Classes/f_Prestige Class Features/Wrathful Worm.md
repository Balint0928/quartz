draft: false

While you have the Wrathful Charge option of the [[Riders' Digest]] feat selected:
- Increase your critical threshold for the attack by 1
- Deal extra weapon damage on a hit that equals your total character level

Even if you don't have this feature selected, you can use it this way, once per short rest.
