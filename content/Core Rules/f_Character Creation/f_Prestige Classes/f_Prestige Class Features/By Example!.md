Whenever you would critically hit an enemy you increase the critical threshold of all allies within 30ft by 1. This lasts until they critically hit, miss an attack, or you are defeated.

Whenever you would defeat an enemy, you increase the weapon damage of all allies within 30ft by an amount equal to your Prestige Class Level. This lasts until they critically hit, miss an attack, or you are defeated.

These effects stack.
