Whenever you would make an attack with a weapon, and you meet the target's [[Dodge Value]]exactly (but do not exceed it), you may use one of the following effects.

- Knock the target [[Prone]]
- Disarm a weapon from one of the opponents hands
- Deal additional damage equal to your current [[Spirit]]
- Move up to your movement speed, without triggering movement based reactions
- Make an attack as a free action with your unarmed strike
