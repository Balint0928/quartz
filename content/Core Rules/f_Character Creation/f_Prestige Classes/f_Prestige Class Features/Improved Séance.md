While a spirit of your choice is active from your [[Séance]] feature, you gain additional benefits:

## Enduring Spirit

At the start of your turn, you can select a single ally within 30ft. That ally takes half damage for 1 round from all sources. You take double damage for 1 round from all sources.

If you are under the effect of the [[Hymn]] feature: You take only 1.5 x damage.

## Wrathful Spirit

Increase the critical threshold of spells with attack rolls (not including ones cast through weapons) by 1.

If you are under the effect of the [[Hymn]] feature: Increase this threshold by 1 more.

## Joyful Spirit

Whenever you would heal an ally with a single target healing effect, choose another ally within 20ft to gain additional healing equal to half the healing you provided. This feature cannot trigger itself.

If you are under the effect of the [[Hymn]] feature: Heal all allies within this area instead.

## Grieving Spirit

Whenever you deal damage with the feature of this spirit, choose one enemy that took this damage, that enemy takes your character level in psychic damage.

If you are under the effect of the [[Hymn]] feature: Deal twice your character level in damage instead.

## Lost Spirit

When you blink out, the space you left explodes with energy, all creatures within 10ft of that explosion are healed or harmed (you can choose individually) an amount equal to your current AP. This damage is psychic damage.

If you are under the effect of the [[Hymn]] feature: This ability also triggers when you blink back from the ethereal plane.
