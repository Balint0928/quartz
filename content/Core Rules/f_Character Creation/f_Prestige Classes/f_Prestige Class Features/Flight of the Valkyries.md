While under effect of the Adrenaline emotion of your [[Lancer Trance]], you can empower your trance as a bonus action and gain the following features:

- You gain a flying speed equal to your movement speed. The distance you fly counts as jumping for your other features.
- You don't provoke [[Opportunity Attack]]s from creatures if you fly out of their reach.
- After you use [[Areal Dive]], as a bonus action you can expend a Hit Die, to summon an Aetheric Echo of yourself which performs the same attack. The echo can land on a any target within 30 feet of you. The same rules apply as for [[Areal Dive]] except that the Echo deals damage that equals your expended hit die + your proficiency bonus.

When your trance ends, and you activated [[Flight of the Valkyries]], you gain a point of [[Exhaustion]].
