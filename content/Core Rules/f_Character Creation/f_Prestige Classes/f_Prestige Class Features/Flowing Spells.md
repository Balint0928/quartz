When you would cast a spell (including a cantrip), you may activate flurry of blows from your [[Focused Spirit]] feature as a free action. You may apply attack roll spells with your unarmed strikes, if the spell is melee they must be through a melee unarmed strike, if the spell is ranged, it must be through a [[Searing Spirit]] ranged attack.
