You lose [[Legacy of Scars]] or [[Glory]] and transforms it into this ability. You retain the HP from [[Legacy of Scars]] and [[Glory]] gained is added as maximum HP to this character, but lose any other effects of Glory.

Legacy of War is a resource pool that is equal to your current Prestige Class Level. You gain points into this pool as per your Legacy of Scars or Glory feature (depending on which one you had before)
At the start of each combat encounter, you may choose:
- Attack Rolls
- Saving Throws
- DV
- [[Trance]] Rage damage
- [[Battle Hardened]]
You gain the maximum of your [[Legacy of War]] as a bonus to that statistic. This bonus lasts as long as you are conscious and not in the [[dying]] condition, after that the effect is lost.
