When you would use your [[Aether Channel]] feature, in addition to its usual effects, you gain the ability to call on a spirit to possess and aid you. Choose one of the following spirits to possess you:

## Enduring Spirit

Whenever you would gain temporary hit points you gain twice as much.

## Wrathful Spirit

Whenever you would damage an enemy with a melee attack, you deal additional damage equal to your character level in psychic damage.

## Joyful Spirit

Whenever you would heal an ally, you heal for an additional amount equal to your character level. This can only apply to single target healing.

## Grieving Spirit

Whenever you would take damage, all enemies within 15ft of you take your spellcasting ability modifier as psychic damage.

## Lost Spirit

In combat, at the end of your turn, gain the effects of the Blink spell.
