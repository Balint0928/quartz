You gain the [[Tactician]] feat. If you already have it, instead you augment it with the following:

You may bathe in the blood of an enemy you triggered Tactician on with a critical hit this turn as a bonus action. If you do, all allies deal additional weapon damage on their next attack equal to your Prestige Class Level. You may only use this once per enemy.
