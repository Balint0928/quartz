You gain 3 extra mantras that you can use with your [[Mantras]] feature.

# Mantra of the Sanctum 
While Mantra of the Sanctum is active, you can use [[Dance of Spirits]] any number of times, but each time you do, reduce your current [[Spirit]] by 1.

# Mantra of the Hungry Spirit
While Mantra of the Hungry Spirit is active, you gain AP as [[Spirit]] instead.

# Mantra of the Unending Watch
- Trigger: You would be killed while Unending Watch is active.
- You are immediately returned to life, regain HP that equals twice your current AP.
- Once this feature triggers, it cannot be used again until you finish an [[Extended Rest]].
