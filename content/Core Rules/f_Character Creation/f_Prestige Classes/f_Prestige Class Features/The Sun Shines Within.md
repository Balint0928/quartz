You are always under the effects of the spell Daylight. This is a supernatural ability.

While you and your allies within 30 feet are within bright light, they gain regeneration of an amount equal to your Charisma modifier plus your proficiency modifier. This ability does not function if you are [[Unconscious]] or [[Dying]]. The regeneration happens at the start of a turn.
