You gain the service of a juvenile Sultan Worm, that loyally serves as your mount. Unlike Sultan Worms sold as regular mounts, this worm has its tail stinger intact, and wont attempt to submerge when you ride it onto open sand. The worm is a combat steed. 

While you are riding your worm, it gains the following benefits:

- The worm gains +1 hit die, +1 AC, +1 AV and +1 STR for each level you gain in this class beyond the first.
- The worm uses your proficiency bonus for its attack rolls
- The worm gains 1 death saving throw attempt

If your Sultan Worm mount is slain, you can obtain a new mount after a day of searching the desert.
