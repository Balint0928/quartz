When you take this prestige class, your [[Sacred Oath]] feature changes into this feature, depending on your [[Patron]]. 

### The Fiend - Ad Infernus
Tenets:
- You must never commit a purely good act, such as giving something solely out of charity, casting a good spell, or using a good item.
- You must never put another person's needs before your own, and you must never put your own needs before those of your deity. Though you can perform acts others might consider helpful, it must be done with the expectation that it ultimately furthers your own goals or those of your master.
- You must further the goals of the Hells, your patron, and ultimately The Great Mother, Dhassyl.
*Fealty Feature:*
**Soul Magnate** - You can use your soul coins from the Infernal Mint feature of your patron in an extra way. When you would fail a Death Saving throw, you can expend a soul coin to succeed instead.

*Fealty Feature:*
**Drink Deep the Madness** - You can use your maddening whispers feature an amount of times that equals your proficiency bonus. For each creature suffering from short term madness within 60 feet of you, the bonuses of your [[Aetherial Arms]] increase by 1.

### The Fey - Ad Maiestus
Tenets:
- Be graceful and majestic with everything you do
- Trick, deceive, mesmerize to your heart's content
- Protect nature and the Fey dwelling within
- Cake

*Fealty Feature:*
**Mushroom Circle** - When you use your Waltz of the Faerie feature, you also become [[invisible]] until the end of your next turn. Attacking or casting a spell breaks this invisibility.

### The Celestial - Ad Caeleste
Tenets:
- You cannot willingly cause chaos, prolong suffering or be the cause of needless deaths
- You must uphold order, law and justice
- You cannot willingly commit evil and depraved acts

*Fealty Feature:*
**Divine Smite** - Instead of healing an ally with your Healing Light feature, you can instead use the same principles when you hit a creature with a weapon attack. Instead of healing the creature, you can deal damage to them.

### The Undead - Ad Mortuus
Tenets:
 - Increase the influence of your master
 - Don't trust the living, they tell tales
 - Seek immortality 
 - Seek power
 - Sow terror in the hearts of the living

*Fealty Feature:*
**Macabre Appetite** - While you are under the effects of your Form of Dread, you can consume the flesh of the dead to sustain your own body. You can, as an action feast on a corpse that is no older than 1 minute. When you do so, increase the permanent AP you gain from Form of Dread, and roll up to two hid dice and regain HP from them (add your con to each roll)
