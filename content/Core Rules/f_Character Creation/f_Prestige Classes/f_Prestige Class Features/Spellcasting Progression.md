If your base class has the [[Spellcasting]] feature, you continue advancing your spell levels and spells as if you were gaining levels in that class.

(For [[Warlock]], you continue having 2 spell slots of the highest available level. 2 4th level slots at 7th, 2 5th level slots at 9th)

(If your base class has the [[Spellcasting]] feature, and you would gain the [[Warlock]]'s [[Occult Arcanum]] feature, you don't get the Pact Magic casting feature)

| Level | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
| ----- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6     | 4   | 3   | 3   | -   | -   |
| 7     | 4   | 3   | 3   | 1   | -   |
| 8     | 4   | 3   | 3   | 2   | -   |
| 9     | 4   | 3   | 3   | 3   | 1   |
| 10    | 4   | 3   | 3   | 3   | 2   |
If your base class does NOT have the [[Spellcasting]] feature, you take your secondary class's spellcasting progression, as if you were starting at level 1 in that class.
