When you succeed on a [[Martial Knowledge]] check, you gain an extra success, granting you an extra piece of information about the target. If you have nothing else left to reveal about a target, a successful [[Martial Knowledge]] check grants you a +2 bonus to your attack rolls, [[Dodge Value]] or Saving throws against that creature until the start of your next turn.

If you are a [[Monk]], you gain a point of [[Spirit]], when you succeed on a [[Martial Knowledge]] check.
