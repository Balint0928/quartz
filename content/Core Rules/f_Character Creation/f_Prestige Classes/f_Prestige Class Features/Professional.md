You gain proficiency with a [[Tools and Masteries|Tool]], if you have proficiency, gain that items special, if you have that, gain that items mastery, if you have that you have advantage on rolls with that item.

Additionally, you may use your [[Tools and Masteries|Tool]] at any time as an interaction on your turn. You may not use this ability more then once per turn.
