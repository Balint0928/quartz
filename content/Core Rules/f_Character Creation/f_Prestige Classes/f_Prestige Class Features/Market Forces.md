You gain the following benefits:

- Your party gains yet another ally slot.
- When you would start your turn without any Coins, you can immediately spend GP to turn it into up to 5 Coins as a bonus action.
- If a creature you used your Hostile Takeover option on, is killed before the start of your next turn, all of your spent Coins for that feature are refunded to you.
- If a creature under the effect of your [[Loan Shark]] feature would be reduced to 0 HP before you regain your HP, you can instead opt out of receiving your HP, and use a reaction to restore the creature to half the amount of HP you would have gained back. A creature benefiting from this effect gains no [[Exhaustion]] from being reduced to 0 HP.
