As an Action or Bonus Action, you can select an ally within 30 feet of you that has the [[Dying]]condition. You expend a point of Divine Focus to grant one of the following boons:

- Law: The target is healed by an amount that equals half of your total character level.
- Chaos: The target can immediately roll a Death Saving throw. This save cannot be influenced any other way. If they succeed, they are healed by an amount that equals your total character level.
