Your [[Dance of Spirits]] feature now provokes the ire of your enemies. When you target an enemy with your [[Dance of Spirits]], it also counts as a use of [[Provoke]]. If you do not have access to it, you can still use it this way a number of times per combat that equal to half your proficiency bonus.

Additionally, whenever you take the [[Dodge Action]], you can also activate [[Dance of Spirits]].
