You may as a Reaction, add a penalty or a bonus to your attack roll when you make an attack. The number for either is up to your current Focus. If you add this as a bonus, you reduce the total damage you deal by an equal amount for the attack, if you reduce the attack, you add additional damage to the total damage of the attack equal to the penalty on the attack roll.
