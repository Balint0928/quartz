Your [[Battle Banner]] is able to provide additional bonuses to your comrades while they can see you.

Dependent on where the banner is equipped, you and all allies within 60 ft gain a +1 to attack, DV or Saving throws while your banner is equipped and you are not [[unconscious]] or in the [[Dying]] condition.

- If attached to a two handed weapon the banner provides a +1 to attack rolls
- If attached as a cloak, the banner provides a +1 to Saving throws
- If attached as a tabard, the banner provides a +1 to DV

If wielded in 1 hand as a weapon, all three of the above bonuses are provided.

For one use of your [[Inspiration]], you can expand the range of your Banner to affect an entire army unit.
