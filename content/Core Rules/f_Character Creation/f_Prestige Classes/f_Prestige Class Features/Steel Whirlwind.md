draft: false

As an action, you can expend a use of your [[Dervish Dance]], and make any number of attacks against creatures up to your Martial level within the reach of your melee weapons. Make separate attacks against each target. Each successful hit increases the critical threshold for the next attack of your Whirlwind by 1. This increase resets when the last attack is made in your current activation of your whirlwind, or you roll a critical hit.
