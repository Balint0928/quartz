A number of times per [[short rest]] equal to your Charisma modifier, you may select an enemy you can see as a Bonus Action. That enemy takes additional radiant or necrotic (depending on [[Contrition]]) damage equal to your number of [[Sneak Attack]] dice, when hit with a weapon attack. This lasts until the start of your next turn.
