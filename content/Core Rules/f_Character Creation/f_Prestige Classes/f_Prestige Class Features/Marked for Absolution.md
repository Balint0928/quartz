A number of times per [[short rest]] equal to your Charisma modifier, you may select an enemy you can see as a Bonus Action. That enemy takes additional radiant or necrotic (depending on your sneak attack damage) damage equal to your number of [[Sneak Attack]] dice from all attacks for until the start of your next turn.
