You may spend a point of Merry and select an enemy you can see. That enemy must make a DC (8+prof+Prestige Class Level) Charisma saving throw or the next d20 roll they make is a natural 1. You can choose a type of d20 roll that is not affected. Such as attack roll, saving throw and skill check.
