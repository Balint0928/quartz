If you used [[Stall]] during your last turn, if you jump at least 30 feet as part of your movement, and are wielding a polearm, you can use your action, to crash down on a creature. The creature must succeed on a Dexterity Save or be knocked prone and take damage equal to quadruple the damage of your weapon (On hit effects still apply). On a successful save, they take only half damage, and are not knocked prone (on hit effects don't apply). You then land in an unoccupied space adjacent to the creature. 

Using High Dive grants you a point of [[Exhaustion]].
