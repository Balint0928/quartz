You gain proficiency with [[Martial Knowledge]] checks. If you already have proficiency in it, double your proficiency bonus on [[Martial Knowledge]] checks.

Additionally, choose one of the following skills in which you have proficiency:
- Arcana
- History
- Nature
- Religion
You gain the [[Skill Mastery]] of that skill, and you can double your proficiency modifier when rolling a check with that skill.
