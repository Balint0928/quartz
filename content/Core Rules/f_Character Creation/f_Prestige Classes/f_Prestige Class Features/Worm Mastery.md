Your mastery of riding your worm mount manifests in the following bonuses:
- You can have an additional option selected for [[Riders' Digest]]
- Gain immunity to Poison damage and the [[Poisoned]] condition
- Whenever your mount lands a critical hit, you can immediately make a weapon attack against the same creature (if it is within range) as a free action. This also triggers vice-versa.
