A samurai's powers come from their resolve and inner focus. You have a maximum amount of Ketsui points that equals your proficiency bonus + your wisdom modifier. 

If you have the [[Spirit]] feature, your spirit pool and your spirit is replaced by this feature. 
If you have the [[Glory]] feature, you can, during a short rest, exchange Glory to [[Ketsui]] in a 2:1 ratio.

By default, you can use a point of [[Ketsui]] to gain advantage on a saving throw. By spending 3 points, you can even gain advantage on a Death saving throw.

You can gain Ketsui the following ways:
- Rolling max on a weapon damage die - Restores 1
- Reducing an enemy to 0 HP - Restores 1
- Rolling a natural 20 on an ability check, attack roll or saving throw - Restores 2
- Finishing an [[Extended Rest]] - Restores all
