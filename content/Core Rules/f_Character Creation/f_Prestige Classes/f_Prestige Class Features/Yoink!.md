Whenever you deal [[Sneak Attack]] damage to an enemy, you may as a Bonus Action attempt to steal an unwielded item from the target. This is a Sleight of Hand opposed roll against the enemy’s Perception. If you possess the [[Skill Mastery]] for Sleight of Hand, you can steal two items instead.
