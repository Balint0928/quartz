You can push yourself over your limit, even if that means risking death. If you would spend a use of [[Dervish Dance]] but have no more uses left, you can instead gain 1d4 levels of [[Exhaustion]] instead.

Additionally, your [[Dervish Dance]] gains the following improvements:
- Your [[Dodge Value]] increases by 4.
- You can make an additional attack, when you take the [[Attack Action]].
- If you enter the [[Dying]] condition, you don't go [[Prone]], however your movement speed bonuses from this prestige class don't apply.
