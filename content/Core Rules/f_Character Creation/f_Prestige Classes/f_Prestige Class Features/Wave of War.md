Whenever you would cast a Spell you may also make an attack with your [[Tome of War]]. This attack may target an enemy within 100ft, if it is not in melee range the attack takes the form of a wave of force that strikes the target instead. 

This attack has its weapon damage converted into force damage and this spell however has its targets changed to affect only the target of the attack.
