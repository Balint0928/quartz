- You gain proficiency with a [[Poisoner's Supplies]], if you have proficiency, gain its special, if you have that, gain its mastery, if you have that you have advantage on rolls with that item.
- You may use your [[Poisoner's Supplies]] at any time as an interaction on your turn. You may not use this ability more then once per turn.
