Once per long rest while you are in the [[Dying]] condition, at the start of your turn you can activate this ability. If you do, you heal to half of your maximum hit points and deal necrotic or radiant damage (based on [[Aetherial Arms]]) equal to this amount to all enemies within 30ft. When you use this ability you may stand up and re-equip your items as free action.
