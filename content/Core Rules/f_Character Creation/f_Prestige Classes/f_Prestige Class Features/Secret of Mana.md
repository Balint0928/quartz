All AP you gain (except for permanent) can be treated as Temporary AP.

- Temporary AP does not stack, you always replace your current with a higher amount of Temporary AP.
- Unless otherwise stated, Temporary AP is cleared after a [[Short Rest]] or a [[Long Rest]].
- When you are reducing AP, reduce Temporary AP first.
