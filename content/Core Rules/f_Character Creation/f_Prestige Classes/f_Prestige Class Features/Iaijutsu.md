For a Samurai, a fight is as much violence as it is a form of art. When you use any of your [[Kata]], you save the type (or Keijo) of the used [[Kata]]. You can have a maximum 3 Keijo saved this way, and you lose all of them if you enter the [[Dying]] condition, become [[Incapacitated]], or if you don't gain a new Keijo for a turn. You can use your saved up Keijo to activate the following features. The order of acquisition matters when using the Keijo.

### Wings of the Free
Keijo: Crane, Crane
As a bonus action, gain flying speed equal to your walking speed until the end of your next turn.

### Tenka Goken
Keijo: Crane, Sakura, Koi
As an action, charge up your blade and unleash a series of waves of power at targets in range. You select up to 3 creatures in a 30ft cone in front of you and make a weapon attack against them. You may attack the same creature multiple times.

### Third Eye
Keijo: Koi, Sakura, Koi
When you are the target of an attack, as a reaction you may react to the incoming danger. Gain resistance to all incoming damage types from that attack that equals your character level.

### Meikyo Shisui
Keijo: Koi, Crane, Sakura
The [[Ketsui]] cost of your next three [[Kata]] will be reduced by 1.
