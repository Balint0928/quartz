You gain additional ways to spend your Coins:

- **Deal Maker:**
	*5 Coins*
	Whenever you attempt to purchase an item, you may reduce the item's cost by 10%, but you and your party must pay 10% more for all other items purchased from that same merchant. By spending at least 5 coins, this changes to 20-20%. This effect persists for 1 week, and cannot stack with itself.
- **Human Resources:**
	*5 Coins*
	Your party gains another ally slot. If you spend at least 5 coins when acquiring a new ally, such as a mercenary, you can hire them 40% cheaper. 
- **Secure the Market:**
	*5 Coins*
	When a creature makes an attack against you, you can spend 5 coins to increase your DV by 1 until the start of your next turn. You can spend more increments of 5 coins, to further increase your DV.
- **Hostile Takeover:**
	*2 Coins*
	When you use your Money Shot feature from [[Pay to Play]], you can spend additional increments of 2 coins, to increase the extra damage, up to a maximum amount that equals your CHA modifier.
- **Golden-Tongued**
	When you use your Silver-Tongued option from your [[Pay to Play]] feature, the amount of Coins you can spend increases to 10, so does your bonus received.
