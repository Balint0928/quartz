When you cast a spell on an ally, you can spend a point of Divine Focus, and gain one of the following effects:

- Law: Ally affected by your spell gains the temporary hit points of your [[Veil of Mana]] feature.
- Chaos: Ally affected by your spell gains an amount of bonus to its next damage roll that equals your temporary hp gain from [[Veil of Mana]].
