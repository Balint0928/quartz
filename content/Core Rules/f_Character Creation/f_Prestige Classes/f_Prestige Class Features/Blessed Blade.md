Your faith in the power of good means you are always benefiting from the effects of the [[Bless]] spell as a supernatural effect.

If you possess the [[Aetherial Arms]] feature, you also benefit from the effects of the Divine Favor spell.

Neither of the above effects require concentration, but they cease to function on unholy ground or similar effects.
