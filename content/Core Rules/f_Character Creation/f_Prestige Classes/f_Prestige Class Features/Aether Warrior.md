You gain 1 permanent AP.
You may use your spellcasting attribute instead of the normal statistics for your weapons. However, for the purposes of qualifying for [[Heft]] your casting attribute counts as 1 lower. You deal additional damage with your weapon equal to half of your current Exposure.

If you would have a feature that grants similar effects such as the Song Weaver [[Bardic College]], you do not gain this feature.
