You have a number of enchantments you can place on your arrows that equal your INT+DEX modifiers each [[Long Rest]]. 

- **Curving Shot:** You may attack an enemy you cannot see but are aware of the position of. This ignores Cover. They must still be in your range however.
- **Blasting Shot:** Your arrow does an additional 2d6 force damage to all adjacent enemies of the target of your attack.
- **Halting Shot:** Your arrow transforms into shadowy tendrils that reduce the targets movement speed to 0 for 1 round. A successful dexterity saving throw negates this effect. (DC is 8 + Prof + INT or DEX)
- **Thrusting Shot:** Your arrow slams into your target knocking them back 30 feet. A successful strength saving throw negates this effect. (DC is 8 + Prof + INT or DEX)
- **Aiding Shot:** Your arrow targets an ally, applying the spell effect on the arrow to them instead and causing no damage.
