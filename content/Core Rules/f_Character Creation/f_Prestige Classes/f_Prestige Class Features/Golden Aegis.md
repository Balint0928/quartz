Your shield gains the following benefits:

- You count your Charisma modifier into your calculations for [[Vigor]].

- Your [[Shield]]'s [[Armor Value]] reduces magical damage as well.

- While wielding a weapon that requires two hands to wield, you gain half of the [[Dodge Value]] and [[Armor Value]] benefits of your shield (but no other shield benefits). Only one such shield can provide this benefit, and you can change the shield during a [[Short Rest]].
