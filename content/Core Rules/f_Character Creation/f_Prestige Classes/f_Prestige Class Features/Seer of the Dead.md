When you finish a short rest, where you used [[Séance]], you can gain one of the following benefits, until the end of your next [[Séance]].

- When you would cast an Enchantment spell you [[Hard Cast]], increase its DC by 2.

- When you would deal psychic damage with a spell you [[Hard Cast]], increase the damage die by one step.

- Gain the effects of the Speak With Dead spell, as a supernatural effect. You can use those questions on any number of dead bodies. If you run out of questions, you can still ask, and gain 2 AP for each extra question.

- When you would cast a Necromancy cantrip, you ignore resistances to the damage of the cantrip.
