Whenever an enemy within range of your Weapon would attempt to do the following, you may make an attack against them as a free action. If you deal damage to them with this attack, you interrupt their attempt and they fail to take the action.

- Use a consumable

- Activate an item (Such as an implement)

- Use a tool

- Don or Doff a Shield

- Sheathe a Weapon
