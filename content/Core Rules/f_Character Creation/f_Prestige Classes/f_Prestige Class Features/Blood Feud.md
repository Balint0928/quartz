As a bonus action, you may spend a point of your [[Legacy of War]], and select an enemy that you can see. That enemy becomes marked by you. If that enemy attacks another target that is not you or includes you, you may attack them with a weapon you are wielding as a free action. This can trigger for each attack that creature makes. This mark lasts for 1 minute.
