title: Visions of Venom
draft: false
tags:
  - class_feature
---
As an Action, you may select an enemy that is currently suffering from the effects of a poison, that creature must make another save against the poison that is afflicting them, they have disadvantage on this saving throw. If they fail, all of their allies within 20ft, take the damage that the original target suffered from its poison. If the poison deals no damage they instead suffer from the [[Poisoned]] condition until the end of your next turn.
