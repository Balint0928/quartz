title: Mystic Purge
draft: false
tags:
  - class_feature
---
As an action, you can expend 2 Divine Focus points to remove all AP from yourself. Both points must be the same alignment. Depending on the type you gain the following benefit:

- Law: You are healed for an amount of HP, that equals twice the amount of AP purged.
- Chaos: Restore 1 spell slot of a level maximum that equals to 1/3 of the amount of AP purged.
