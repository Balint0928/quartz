You deal an additional 2d6 necrotic or radiant damage on your weapon attacks, based on your chosen damage for [[Aetherial Arms]]. Whenever you would deal radiant damage, you gain a bonus to your next source of necrotic damage equal to your total character level. Whenever you would deal necrotic damage, your next source of radiant damage heals you for half of the damage dealt.
