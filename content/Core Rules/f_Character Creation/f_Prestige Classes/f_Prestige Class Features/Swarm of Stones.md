As your AP increases, rocks and stones begin to levitate around you.

When you gain AP, for each point you gain, you gain a floating stone. You can have a maximum amount of stones that equals your spellcasting ability modifier. When you take damage, or hit by an attack, you lose one stone. Two on a critical hit. If you are hit by Lightning or Thunder damage, you lose all of your stones. While you have stones floating about you, for each stone you gain the following benefits:

- +1 bonus to DV
- +1 damage to weapons dealing bludgeoning damage
- Usable as ammunition for slings and spell effects that use projectiles. If used this way, they deal an additional 1d6 crush damage.

At the end of your turns, your stones are refreshed to your current AP. (Excluding Permanent AP)
