title: Shadow of the Tormentor
draft: false
tags:
  - class_feature
---
You can choose an elemental damage type when you first gain this feature. That damage type becomes Shadow type for you (for example, fire damage becomes shadow fire, cold becomes shadow cold, etc...). If you deal damage with a Shadow type effect you control:

- That damage deals twice the amount to temporary hitpoints.
- That effect counts as an illusion spell.
