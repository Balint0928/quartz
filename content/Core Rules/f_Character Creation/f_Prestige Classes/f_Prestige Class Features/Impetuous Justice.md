title: Impetuous Justice
draft: false
tags:
  - class_feature
---
As an action you can select a single enemy. For 1 round, if that enemy would reduce to 0 hp or critically hit any of your allies, you may add half your maximum hit points as radiant damage on your next attack against them. This does not stack with itself. In addition, you may attack an additional time on your next [[Attack Action]]. You can use this ability once per [[Short Rest]].

If you possess the [[Aetherial Arms]] feature, you may instead add your entire maximum hit points to this attack instead.
