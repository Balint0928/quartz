As an action, you may fire your stones at a target within 30ft. Each stone is a ranged attack using the ranged spell attack modifier of your base class. Each stone does 1d6 bludgeoning damage with a heft equal to your casting modifier. You can choose any number of stones you have, but they are expended if used.
