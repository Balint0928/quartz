Once per long rest, as an action, you can attempt to find a non-weapon or non-armor mundane item in your backpack that you might not even have. You have a base 20% chance of finding the item. If you spend GP of at least half the item's value, the chance increases to 60%. If you pay the full price, the chance increases to 90%. The item should also be able to be pulled from a normal backpack.
