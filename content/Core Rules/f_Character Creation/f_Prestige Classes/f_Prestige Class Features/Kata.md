Your path as a warrior is measured in the many martial arts and techniques you have mastered. Using these techniques requires [[Ketsui]]. As you reach higher levels, you will have access to better, more powerful Katas. When you finish an [[Extended Rest]], you can swap out 2 of your known Katas to another one you meet the requirements for in this list. You can only have an amount of Kata active at a time that equals 1 + your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). Each Kata has a type: Crane, Koi or Sakura. Crane moves are your "base" moves, they usually involve making an attack. Koi moves are your utility moves, that allow you to set up your next moves. Sakura, are moves that need setup or a specific trigger to activate.


> [!tip] Jinpu
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 6
> **Type:** Crane
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1-2
> As an action you make a number of ranged weapon attacks with a range of 30ft. using blades of wind that equals the amount of Ketsui expended. Make these attack and damage rolls as though you are making melee weapon attacks. Attacks made in this way deal thunder damage.


> [!tip] Benkyō
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 6
> **Type:** Crane
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
> As an action you focus on perceiving target's most vital areas as you strike. Make a melee weapon attack, adding your Wisdom modifier to the attack and damage rolls. If the attack hits, you immediately succeed on a [[Martial Knowledge]] check against the target.

> [!tip] Shifu
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 6
> **Type:** Crane
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
> As a bonus action, you can make a [[Light Attack]].

> [!tip] Shūchū
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 6
> **Type:** Koi
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
> As a bonus action you infuse your blade with [[Ketsui]] to deliver swifter strikes. Melee weapon attacks you make until the end of your turn have advantage.


> [!tip] Hanron
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 6
> **Type:** Crane
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
> When a creature attacks you, as a reaction you may make an [[Opportunity Attack]] against the creature.


> [!tip] Shinten
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 6
> **Type:** Sakura
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
> When you make a [[Light Attack]] of any kind, you can make an additional [[Light Attack]]. This feature cannot trigger itself.

> [!tip] Yaten
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 6
> **Type:** Koi
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
> You can take the [[Disengage Action]] as a bonus action. If you have Jinpu active, you can make one attack using it without expending more [[Ketsui]]. However this attack is a [[Light Attack]].

> [!tip] Niju Shi Ho
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 8
> **Type:** Koi
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
>You can take the [[Dash Action]] as a bonus action. Your first attack at the end of your dash is an instant hit.

> [!tip] Gekko
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 8
> **Type:** Crane
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
>You make one melee weapon attack as an action. If you hit an enemy with this attack while flanking it, your next Crane type Kata you use before the end of your next turn, will deal an extra weapon die worth of damage (this increase can apply to [[Light Attack]]s), and have its critical threshold increased by 1.

> [!tip] Kasha
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 8
> **Type:** Koi
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
>You can activate this Kata as a free action when you use [[Provoke]]. All of your attacks until the end of your turn that you make against that target of your [[Provoke]] are considered to be flanking attacks.

> [!tip] Higanbana
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 8
> **Type:** Sakura
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 2
>When you hit a creature you are flanking, they take [[Bleed]] that equals half your character level. This stacks with [[Bleed]] gained from this source or other Katas but not with anything else.

> [!tip] Hagakure
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 9
> **Type:** Sakura
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 2
>When you make an attack, you may grant that attack a bonus or penalty equal to your character level. If you grant a bonus to the attack, you receive the same penalty to your damage of that attack, and vice versa. If this attack defeats an enemy, by dealing the exact amount of remaining HP in damage the target had, the [[Ketsui]] cost of this Kata is refunded.

> [!tip] Enpi
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 9
> **Type:** Koi
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 2
>When you make an attack, you may grant that attack a bonus or penalty equal to your character level. If you grant a bonus to the attack, you receive the same penalty to your damage of that attack, and vice versa. If this attack defeats an enemy, by dealing the exact amount of remaining HP in damage the target had, the [[Ketsui]] cost of this Kata is refunded.


> [!tip] Hissatsu
> **Prerequisite**: Lvl 10
> **Type:** Sakura
> **[[Ketsui]]** cost: 1
>When you defeat an enemy, and you only attacked that creature once this round, you may immediately move up to your movement speed, and attack another creature as a free action.
