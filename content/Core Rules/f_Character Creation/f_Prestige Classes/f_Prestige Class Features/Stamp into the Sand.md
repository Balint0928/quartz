While you have the Trample option of the [[Riders' Digest]] feat selected:
- Your worm mount can use its sting where the feature calls for a hoof attack
- When a creature is knocked prone by your trample, it must use all of its movement speed to stand up

Even if you don't have this feature selected, you can use it this way, once per short rest.
