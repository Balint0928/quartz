You gain proficiency with the [[Net]]. If you have proficiency with this weapon you gain the special, if you have the special you gain the mastery, if you have the mastery, you may wield this weapon and another weapon at the same time as if dual wielding them. In addition, whenever you attack a [[Restrained]] enemy, if the attack exceeds their [[Dodge Value]] by 5 or more, the attack is a critical hit.
