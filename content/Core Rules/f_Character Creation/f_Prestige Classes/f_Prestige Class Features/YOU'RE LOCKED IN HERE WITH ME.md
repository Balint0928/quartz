Once per combat encounter, as a check action, you can make one [[Martial Knowledge]] check. This check is against all enemies currently in the combat. Roll only once. If you succeed, resolve the [[Martial Knowledge]] checks as usual, and for 1 minute, gain +5 to Attack and Damage rolls, Saving Throws and Skill Checks. Additionally, during this effect, your critical threshold increases by an amount equal to your proficiency bonus.
