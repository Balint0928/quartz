You must prepare this attack as a bonus action. When you take the bonus action, your movement becomes 0 until you take the action part of this feature. As an action, you can expend all [[Ketsui]] charges, to start a torrent of attacks in a 20 foot sphere centered upon yourself. Each creature of your choice within this area must succeed on a dexterity saving throw (DC = 8+Prof+WIS) or take an amount of force damage that equals 2d10 x the expended amount of [[Ketsui]]. A creature that succeeds on the saving throw still takes half of the damage. Once you used this ability, you cannot use it again, until you have regained your maximum amount of [[Ketsui]].
