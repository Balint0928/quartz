Whenever you would trigger your [[Heretic's Hunger]], you may do one of the following:

- Deal Necrotic or Radiant damage to all creatures within 30ft. with Corrupted or Hallowed Aether Points, equal to the amount of Tainted AP they possess.

- Reduce your current AP by 1.

- Force a creature within 30ft. of you to succeed on a spellcasting ability saving throw (or Constitution) against DC 10 + your total character level. On a failed save, they gain 2 AP.
