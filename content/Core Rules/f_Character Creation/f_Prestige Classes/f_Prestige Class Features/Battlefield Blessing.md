When you use your [[Lay of the Land]] feature, you can gain a benefit for you and your allies based on the terrain you are in. Expending a point of Divine Focus allows you to use this feature as an action. This effect lasts until your next long rest. Only one blessing can be active at a time.

### Cold
You and your allies gain 1d6 Cold damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Athletics checks.

### Desert
You and your allies gain 1d6 Fire damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Survival checks.

### Forest & Jungle
You and your allies gain 1d6 Poison damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Stealth checks.

### Mountains & Hills
You and your allies gain 1d6 Lightning damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Acrobatics checks.

### Plains
You and your allies gain 1d6 Radiant damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Perception checks.

### Swamp
You and your allies gain 1d6 Necrotic damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Investigation checks.

### Subterranean
You and your allies gain 1d6 Thunder damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Stealth checks.

### Urban
You and your allies gain 1d6 Acid damage bonus on weapon attacks they make. In addition you have advantage on Insight checks.

### Water & Coast
You and your allies ignore penalties for fighting in water. In addition you have advantage on Athletics checks.
