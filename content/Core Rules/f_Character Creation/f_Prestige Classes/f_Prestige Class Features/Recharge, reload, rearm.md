draft: false
tags:

You can use your [[Aether Channel]] feature as an action. When you would regain expended spell slots, you may instead choose to recharge a feature from [[Replicate Revelation]]. You regain charges in a 1:1 spell slot level to charge ratio. If the feature cannot recharge, or has maximum charges, you may instead immediately switch out your currently copied feature to another one you have learned this combat through [[Replicate Revelation]] and then regain one charge of that ability if you can.
