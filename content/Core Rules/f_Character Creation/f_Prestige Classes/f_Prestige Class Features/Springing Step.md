While your [[Dervish Dance]] is active, you ignore non-magical difficult terrain, and gain advantage on STR and DEX checks and saving throws against [[Grappled]] or [[Restrained]].

Additionally, as a bonus action, while under the effects of your [[Dervish Dance]], you can expend a use of your [[Dervish Dance]] to teleport to an unoccupied space within 30 feet of you that you can see.
