You may use your [[Replicate Revelation]] feature when you use [[Arcane Knowledge]] to identify spells. If you do so, you must expend a spell slot that matches the spell's level. If you have no such spell slot, you must expend your highest, and gain a number of AP that equals the difference between the two levels. When you copy a spell, you must use your Action in your turn to cast it, and then the spell fades from your memory. You cannot copy spells with a casting time of Reaction.
