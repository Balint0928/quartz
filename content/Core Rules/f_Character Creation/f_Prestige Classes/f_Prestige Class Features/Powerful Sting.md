While mounted on your worm, once per turn, when you make a weapon attack, the worm can use its reaction to make a Stinger attack against a creature within its range. 

When the worm makes this attack (but before you roll) you can choose to make the attack a Powerful Sting. When doing so, the worm gains a bonus to its attack and damage roll that equals your STR or DEX modifier, the DC for the constitution save increases by your total level in this prestige class and the poison deals an extra die of damage.

The Powerful Sting attack can be used a number of times that equals your total character level per [[Long Rest]]
