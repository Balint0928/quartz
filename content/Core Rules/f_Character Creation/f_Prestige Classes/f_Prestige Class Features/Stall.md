While you are in the Adrenaline emotion of [[Lancer Trance]], when you reach the peak of any jump, you may choose to pause mid air, in place up until the end of your next turn.

You may perform actions as normal while suspended in the air, and on your following turn may move as though you are jumping when coming back down. Using [[Stall]] and not attacking or taking damage won't break your [[Lancer Trance]]. 

You may only use stall once and may not use it again until you have landed on the ground or similar surface.
