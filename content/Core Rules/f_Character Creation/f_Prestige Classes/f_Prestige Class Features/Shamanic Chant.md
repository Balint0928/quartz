Whenever you would be healed by your [[Blood of the Fallen]] feature, depending on the type of Divine Focus used, you gain the following benefit:

- Law: The next healing spell you cast restores an additional amount of HP that equals the same amount you regained from [[Blood of the Fallen]].
- Chaos: The next damaging spell you cast deals an additional amount of damage that equals the same amount you regained from [[Blood of the Fallen]]

You can only have one of these in effect and they do not stack.
