As an action, you can select an enemy you can see. That enemy must succeed on a spellcasting ability saving throw (or Constitution) against DC 10 + your total character level. On a failed roll, they take 10 Necrotic or Radiant damage per point of Corrupted or Hallowed AP they possess.
