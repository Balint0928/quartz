All enemies that gain AP within 100ft. of you gain that as [[Tainted Aether]]. If an enemy would get to 10 (or its own maximum) Corrupted or Hallowed AP, they cannot trigger features and effects like Apotheosis or an Aether Event. Instead, they take 50 points of Necrotic or Radiant damage (based on their Taint type), and their AP is reduced to their base level. When this effect occurs it triggers your [[Heretic's Hunger]].
