As an action, you can expend your stones to call upon a rockslide to affect creatures of your choice within 20ft of you. For each stone expended, these enemies take 1d8 bludgeoning damage. A successful strength saving throw (DC 8+Prof+Prestige Class Level) halves this damage. The area you summoned this in is considered difficult terrain for 1 minute. You are immune to this effect. You can use this feature a number of times between long rests, that equals your proficiency modifier.
