Your study of the secrets of magic and Aether allows you to use hidden techniques. You can employ these secrets of magic based on the spell school of a spell you cast. You may use this feature a number of times that equals your INT + CHA modifiers per [[Long Rest]].

### Abjuration
Whenever you cast an abjuration spell you gain a bonus to your DV equal to the level of the spell until the end of your next turn.

### Conjuration
Whenever you would cast a conjuration spell, you may teleport 5ft per level of the spell cast, to an unoccupied space you can see. If the spell is already teleporting you, you add this to the distance you teleport.

### Divination
Whenever you would cast a divination spell, you gain advantage on your next saving throw or attack roll you make.

### Enchantment
Whenever you would cast an Enchantment spell, one enemy within 30ft must make the spell save of that spell (even if it doesn't have one), if they fail they gain disadvantage on the next saving throw or attack roll they make.

### Evocation
Whenever you would cast an evocation spell, all enemies within 20ft of you take the spells level in damage as force damage.

### Illusion
Whenever you would cast an Illusion spell, you gain [[Invisibility]] for a number of rounds equal to the spell level. If that spell would grant you invisibility, you instead gain Greater invisibility for this duration.

### Necromancy
Whenever you would cast a necromancy spell, you heal yourself an amount equal to the level of the spell. If an enemy that was affected by your necromancy spell would be defeated within 1 round, you instead heal an amount equal to your INT + CHA modifier.

### Transmutation
Whenever you would cast a transmutation spell, you gain a bonus to an Ability Score, equal to the spell level you cast until the end of your next turn.
