When at the start of your turn you are afflicted with one of these conditions:
- [[Bleed]]
- [[Blinded]]
- [[Burn]]
- [[Charmed]]
- [[Deafened]]
- [[Frightened]]
- [[Grappled]]
- [[Incapacitated]]
- [[Paralyzed]]
- [[Petrified]]
- [[Poisoned]]
- [[Restrained]]
- [[Stunned]]
You can spend 1 [[Ketsui]] to immediately end the effect on yourself as a free action. However, you gain 1 [[Doomed]]. [[Doomed]] gained from this feature is cleared at the end of a [[Long Rest]].
