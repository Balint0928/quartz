Whenever the [[dying]] condition ends on you, you may stand up as a free action. This standing up does not cost you any movement. In addition, if your next attack roll hits its target, it automatically counts as a critical hit.

Furthermore, when you roll death saving throws, the threshold for a critical success increases by an amount equal to your current [[Legacy of War]].
