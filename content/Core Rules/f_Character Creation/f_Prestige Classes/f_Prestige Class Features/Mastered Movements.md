You are so sure of your movements that you are unaffected by adverse conditions. When you roll a check for jumping (Acrobatics or Athletics), Dancing (Performance) or any contested checks against being knocked [[Prone]], you can, instead of rolling, take 10 as a result on the d20.

Additionally, while you are not wearing a heavy armor or wielding a shield, your movement speed increases by 5 feet.
