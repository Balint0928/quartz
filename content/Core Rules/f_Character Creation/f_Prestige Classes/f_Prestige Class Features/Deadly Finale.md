Once per combat you may erupt with a wave of thunderous drums. All enemies within 30ft must make a constitution saving throw DC (8+Prof+Con+Cha+PL). If they fail they take 10d8 thunder damage and are [[Stunned]] for 1 round. If they succeed they take half damage instead.
