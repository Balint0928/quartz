While you are under the effect of the Adrenaline emotion of [[Lancer Trance]] and wielding a [[Lance]], you gain the following benefits:
- You no longer have disadvantage against enemies within 5 feet of you.
- If you have the [[Weapon Mastery]] with the [[Lance]], it also gains the [[Impale]] and [[Deep Bite]] features.
