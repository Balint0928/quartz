Your [[Arcane Grimoire]] transforms into a melee weapon of your choice with which you are proficient with. This weapon is made of pure Aether, and is covered in sigils and runes representing your spells. This weapon functions as your Grimoire and Spellcasting Focus.

You lose the spell research function of the Grimoire, and instead this item gains the ability to be enchanted as a weapon, upgraded as a Grimoire as if you were gaining [[Wizard]] levels, and could function as any spellcasting focus for the purpose of their masteries. (This choice is made when you first manifest your tome of war.)
