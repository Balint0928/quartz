When you use [[Dance of Spirits]] on an ally, that ally gains the benefit of your [[Blessing of the Chosen]]. Only one ally can be affected by this ability at a time, and the ally uses their own AP for calculating the benefits. If you are under the effects of your [[Hymn]], its increased benefits also apply to the ally.
