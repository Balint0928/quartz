When you [[Hard Cast]] a spell that has an attack roll, and you roll a prime number on the d20, (2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19) you can grant that spell an extra effect from this list:

- You gain temporary hp that equals half the amount of damage you dealt with the spell.
- It causes half of the damage you dealt again to the target at the start of the target's next turn.
- You regain an expended spell slot of a level that is lower than the spell cast, as a Temporary Spell Slot. This temporary slot is lost at the end of the current combat encounter.
