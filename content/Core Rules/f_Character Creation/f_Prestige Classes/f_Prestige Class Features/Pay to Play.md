You can use your Coins created by [[Personal Mint]], to fuel the following abilities:

- **Money Shot:**
	2 Coin
	When you make an attack, you can spend 1 Coin to have your attack deal 1 extra weapon damage die. You can use this feature once per attack. If you have the [[Sneak Attack]] feature, the extra damage die can be a [[Sneak Attack]] die.
- **Health Insurance:**
	2 Coins
	As a bonus action, grant an ally that you can see 1d6 Temporary HP
- **Firm Handshake:**
	3 Coins
	As a bonus action, grant an ally that you can see 1d4 bonus to their next attack roll or ability check.
- **Appraisal:**
	8 Coins
	You can accurately predict the value of an item you are holding. During a 1 minute ritual, you can spend 8 Coins to identify a magical item or potion.
- **Pocket Change:**
	5 Coins
	As a bonus action, you can spend 5 coins, and throw it at a creature within 20 feet of you. The next attack against the target is made from advantage.
- **Silver-tongued:**
	5 Coins
	When you make a CHA skill check, you can spend up to 5 Coins, to increase the outcome of your roll with the same amount.
