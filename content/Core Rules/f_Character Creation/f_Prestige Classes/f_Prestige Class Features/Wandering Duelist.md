draft: false

You have proficiency with only light armor and lose proficiency with other armors and shields. You gain 1 extra AV to your armor. If you have the weapon special of the weapon you are using, increase this bonus to 2. If you have the mastery, increase it to 3.

Additionally, whenever you would attack an enemy, you gain +1 to your [[Dodge Value]] against the next attack that is made against you.
