As an action, you can combine your stones into one large boulder and strike out at your enemies. This boulder deals 1d10 points of crush damage per stone used. This ability affects one target within 60ft. That target must make a DC (8+Prof+Prestige Class Level) Strength saving throw, if they succeed they take half damage. If they fail, they take full damage and are stunned for 1 round. This counts as a siege weapon for special damage purposes.  You can use this feature a number of times that equals your proficiency per long rest.
