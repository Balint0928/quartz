Whenever you cast a spell, you may cast that spell through your arrow. You use the arrow as the center point for the spells radius if it has one. If it is an attack spell, you apply it to the target of your ranged attack instead. 

Additionally, any enchantment you have on the arrows or on your ranged weapons also apply to your spells cast this way.
