You gain access to the [[Barbarian]]'s [[Trance]] feature, and can only use this following option:
- Adrenaline: Your long jump is up to 30 feet and your high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start. You don't take fall damage when landing from your own jumps, and only take half the amount from other fall damage. 

If you already have the [[Trance]] feature from another source (for example if you are a [[Barbarian]]) the Adrenaline option is added to your list of emotions. If you have the [[Complex Emotions]] feature, selecting Adrenaline as your second option won't give you [[Exhaustion]] if your [[Trance]] ends.
