While traveling overland, you may spend a point of Divine Focus when a random hostile encounter happens.

- Law: You ignore the encounter, bypassing it immediately.

- Chaos: You may automatically attempt to ambush the hostiles. 

If you are in the area of your [[Battlefield Blessing]], the Divine Focus point is not spent. (You still need to have at least one)
