Your [[Hymn]] and [[Chant]] features are combined into this ability, and the parameters of both abilities change to the following. You may use this ability as an action once per short rest. For the next minute, you gain the following benefits:

- DCs of your spells you cast increase by 2
- Damage of spells you cast are increased by your current AP
- Every spell (including cantrips) you cast by expending a spell slot, also allows you to cast a cantrip as a free action. Once per turn.
- You regain a point of Divine Focus at the start of the duration
