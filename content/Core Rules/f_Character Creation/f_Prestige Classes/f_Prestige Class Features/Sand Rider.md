Your bond with your worm mount becomes legendary, and enables you to follow the beast when it dives beneath the sand (dust or loose earth) or uses its Evasive Diver feature (when using the latter, you also avoid the incoming damage). You can travel in this manner for 10 minutes at a time before you and your mount must surface for air. While beneath the sand the mount uses its burrow speed.

You cannot remain mounted if the worm burrows into solid ground.
