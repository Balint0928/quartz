## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: You must be a level 5 [[Paladin]] or [[Warlock]]*

- Hit dice: [[Paladin]] gets 1d8, [[Warlock]] gets 1d10

# Features by level
**Level 6**
- Gain the [[Mana of War]]
- [[Otherworldly Fealty]]
- [[Paladin]] gets: [[Spellcasting]], [[Occult Arcanum]]
- [[Warlock]] gets: [[Spellcasting]], [[Aetherial Arms]], [[Sacred Oath]]

**Level 7**
- [[Paladin]] gets: [[Heretic's Hunger]], [[Eldritch Invocation]]
- [[Warlock]] gets: [[Oathbound Vigilance]]

**Level 8**
- [[Sacred Covenant]]

**Level 9**
- [[Relentless Envoy]]

**Level 10**
- [[To Serve Beyond Death]]
