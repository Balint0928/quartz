## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: You have the [[Martial]] role*

Dervishes are warriors who thrive in the chaos of battle. They weave through hordes of enemies, dancing from foe to foe in a deadly rhythm of steel.

- Role progression: [[Martial]]
- Hit dice: 1d10
