## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: Must be a 5th level [[Barbarian]] or [[Fighter]]*

- Hit dice: [[Barbarian]] gains 1d10, [[Fighter]] gains 1d12

# Features by level
**Level 6**
- [[Legacy of War]]
- [[Ancestral Call]]
- [[Barbarian]] gets: [[Second Wind]], [[Warrior’s Soul]]
- [[Fighter]] gets: [[Savage Defense]], [[Danger Sense]]

**Level 7**
- [[Barbarian]] gets: [[Action Surge]]
- [[Fighter]] gets: [[Trance]]

**Level 8**
- [[Spirit Rush]]

**Level 9**
- [[Blood Feud]]

**Level 10**
- [[The Host of War]]
