## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: You must be a level 5 [[Fighter]] or [[Monk]]*

- Hit dice: [[Fighter]] gets 1d8, [[Monk]] gets 1d10

# Features by level
**Level 6**
- [[Wandering Duelist]]
- [[Balancing Blade]]
- [[Fighter]] gets: [[Spirit]], [[Spirited Defense]], [[Martial Arts]]
- [[Monk]] gets: [[Second Wind]], [[Warrior’s Soul]]

**Level 7**
- [[Fighter]] gets: [[Focused Spirit]]
- [[Monk]] gets: [[Action Surge]]

**Level 8**
- [[Perfect Strike]]

**Level 9**
- [[Flowing Spirit]]

**Level 10**
- [[Perfect Tempo]]
