## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: You must be a level 5 [[Wizard]] or [[Bard]]*

- Hit dice: [[Bard]] gains 1d6, [[Wizard]] gains 1d8

# Features by level
**Level 6**
- [[Adept]] (Must choose Arcana, History, Nature or [[Martial Knowledge]])
- [[Arcane Loophole]]
- [[Bard]] gains: [[Spellcasting Progression]], [[Arcane Grimoire]], [[Arcane Knowledge]] proficiency
- [[Wizard]] gains: [[Spellcasting Progression]], [[Inspiration]]

**Level 7**
- [[Bard]] gains: [[Chant]]
- [[Wizard]] gains: [[Maestro]]

**Level 8**
- [[Arcanum Obscurus]]

**Level 9**
- [[Word of Power]]

**Level 10**
- [[Secret of Mana]]
