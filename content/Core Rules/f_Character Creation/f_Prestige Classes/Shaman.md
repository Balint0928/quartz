## Basic info:
*Prerequisite: Must be a 5th level [[Barbarian]] or [[Cleric]]*

- Hit dice: [[Barbarian]] gets 1d8, [[Cleric]] gets 1d12

# Features by level
**Level 6**
- [[Blood of the Fallen]]
- [[Shamanic Chant]]
- [[Barbarian]] gets: [[Spellcasting]], [[Deity]], [[Domain Spells]]
- [[Cleric]] gets: [[Savage Defense]], [[Danger Sense]]

**Level 7**
- [[Barbarian]] gets: [[Hymn]]
- [[Cleric]] gets: [[Trance]]

**Level 8**
- [[Vitality Surge]]

**Level 9**
- [[Reckless Abandon]]

**Level 10**
- [[For Wrath! For Ruin!]]
