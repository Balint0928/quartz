The Lands of Iqor are divided into many regions. For now, these are the largest regions on the continent. Depending on where your character originates from, you gain benefits based on your class roles.

- [[Sleepless Sands]]
- [[Steppes of Azul]]
- [[Sea of Thousand Tears]]
- [[Ankha-Am]]
- [[The Ixalanti]]
- [[The Misted Lands]]
