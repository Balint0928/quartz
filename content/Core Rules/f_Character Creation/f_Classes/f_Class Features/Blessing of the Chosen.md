Your [[Deity]] grants you a minor boon for your dedication. Choose one of these Blessings:

- **Blessing of The Light:** Gain the ability to summon a halo of light around your head as a bonus action, as per the Light spell. While within the bright light of your halo, an allied creature can add half of your current AP to its death saving throws.
- **Blessing of The Free:** Gain three times your current AP as bonus movement speed. (Rounded down to a factor of 5)
- **Blessing of The Sentinel:** You can add half of your current AP to your [[Dodge Value]]. (Max 5 and you cannot be wearing Heavy Armor to benefit from this)
- **Blessing of The Justiciar:** You can add half of your current AP to your attack rolls.
- **Blessing of The Archivist:** You can add half of your current AP to your skill checks.
