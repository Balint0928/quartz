When a creature within 15 feet of you hits you with an attack, as a reaction you can put that foe who dared to harm you in their proper place. The foe must choose one of the following:
- The target kneels, dropping [[Prone]].
- The target refuses to kneel, and takes psychic damage that equals your spellcasting modifier. 
This feature can be used a number of times each combat that equals half your prof bonus.
