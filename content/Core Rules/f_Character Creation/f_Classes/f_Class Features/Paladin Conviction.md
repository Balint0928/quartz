### Arms Master
**Sword Oath:** While your [[Aetherial Arms]] feature is empowering your weapon it glows with a shining light. The weapons emits bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. Additionally you can expend a spell slot (and increase your AP), then add your current AP to the bonus damage from [[Aetherial Arms]], when you hit an enemy with your weapon.

### Great Knight
**Shield Oath:** While your [[Aetherial Arms]] feature is empowering your shield it glows with a shining light. The shield emits bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. Additionally, when [[Aetherial Arms]] is empowering your shield, and you use the [[Guard]]bonus action, you can expend a spell slot (and increase your AP), then add half of your current AP as a bonus to your [[Dodge Value]] instead of half of your proficiency.

### Crusader
**Divine Crusader:** You choose a [[Deity]] as per the [[Cleric]]'s feature. The chosen Deity provides you with 2 of their Divine Focus points. Additionally, you also gain the [[Domain Spells]] feature.
**Stave Oath:** When you cast a spell, you can spend a Divine Focus point instead of a spell slot. 1 point = 1 slot.
