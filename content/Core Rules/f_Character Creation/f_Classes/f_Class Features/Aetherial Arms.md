You are able to fill your equipment with Aether to assist you in battle. As a bonus action you can focus your Aether into your weapon, shield or armor to produce various effects. While an item is infused this way, gain 1 Permanent AP. These effects last for 1 minute.

- **Weapon:** Attacks with your weapon deal bonus radiant or necrotic damage equal to your proficiency bonus.
- **Shield:** Gain bonus [[Dodge Value]] to your shield that equals half your prof bonus (rounded down).
- **Armor:** Gain extra [[Armor Value]] that equals your proficiency bonus.

You can infuse your weapons with Aether a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus. You may only infuse one item at a time. Uses of this feature are refreshed when you complete a [[long rest]].
