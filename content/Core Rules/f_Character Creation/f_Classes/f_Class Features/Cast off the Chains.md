---
title: Cast off the Chains
draft: false
tags:
  - class_feature
  - paladin
---
If a creature within 20 feet of you starts its turn in the [[Grappled]], [[Restrained]] or [[Stunned]] condition, you can use your reaction, to allow them to re-roll their save. They can add your current AP to their save as well. If they succeed, the effect ends on them, and they can immediately move 5 feet in any direction, without triggering movement based reactions.
