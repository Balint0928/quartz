Sorcerers have a wellspring of aether inside of them, marking them as innately magical creatures. This font of aether grants you the following effects:

- You gain 3 permanent AP, that can never be reduced.
- For you, an aether event only happens when you reach 11 AP instead of 10. (This also counts for Apotheosis feats)
- You gain an extra spell slot for each spell level you have access to.
- You add your current AP to all damage rolls of spells you cast using a spell slot. This can only happen once per casting of a spell.
