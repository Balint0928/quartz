draft: false
tags:

You twist the mind of a creature to fill it with thoughts of evil and brutal acts. When a creature makes an attack within 15 feet of you, you can force them to choose:
- Choose an ally as the new target of their attack. If no such creatures fit the criteria, the attack is wasted.
- Take force damage that equals your spellcasting ability modifier.
This feature can be used a number of times each combat that equals half your prof bonus.
