Your patron's power lends you access to magic in a special way.

### Pact Magic
You only ever have two spell slots, these are always cast at the highest spell level possible for the Warlock to cast by the Magic role’s scaling.

 Spell slots per Spell Level:

|Level|1st|2nd|3rd|
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
|1st|2|—|—|
|2nd|2|—|—|
|3rd|—|2|—|
|4th|—|2|—|
|5th|—|—|2|
### Eldritch Blast
You gain a special cantrip from your patron, that is unique to each patron. This cantrip does not count against the number of cantrips known.

- Fiend: [[Infernal Blast]]
- Great Old One: [[Psychic Blast]]
- Fey: [[Elder Blast]]
- Celestial: [[Divine Blast]]
- Undead: [[Ghast Blast]]
