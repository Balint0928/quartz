You learn two spells from the Cleric spell list, that match your deity’s domains and doesn’t collide with their Anathemas.

Additionally, while you have at least one of the following type of Divine Focus, your spells are augmented:

- While you have at least one Chaos focus, cantrips you cast deal extra damage that equals half your current AP
- While you have at least one Law focus, whenever you would heal a creature with a spell, you add half your current AP to the healed amount.
