### Pact of the Blade
- You have 1 permanent AP
- As a Bonus Action, you can conjure a pact weapon in your hand—a Simple or Martial Melee weapon of your choice with which you bond—or create a bond with a magic weapon you touch; you can't bond with a magic weapon if someone else is attuned to it or another Warlock is bonded with it. Until the bond ends, you have proficiency with the weapon, and you can use it as a Spellcasting Focus.
- You may use your spellcasting ability instead of the normal statistics for your weapons. However, for the purposes of qualifying for Heft your casting attribute counts as 1 lower. You deal additional damage with your weapon equal to half of your current AP.
- **Hungering Fury:** When you kill a creature with your pact weapon, you trigger [[Heretic's Hunger]]. This can only happen once per combat encounter.

### Pact of the Tome
- **Book of Shadows:** You can conjure a book of shadow that contains 3 cantrips, and two 1st level spells that have the Ritual tag. You cannot otherwise have these spells prepared. You can change these spells at the end of a short or long rest, and you can use the book to cast these spells. The book also functions as a spellcasting focus.
- **Secrets on the Page:** Whenever you or an ally would critically hit a creature you can see, you trigger [[Heretic's Hunger]] as a reaction.
- When you trigger [[Heretic's Hunger]], you gain advantage on your next [[Arcane Knowledge]] check.

### Pact of the Cannon
- **Eldritch Cannon:** You can conjure a a special eldritch implement which empowers your Eldritch Blasts. The form the implement takes is up to you. You can use the implement as a spellcasting focus. When you create the cannon, and every long rest after, you can choose two of the features below to enhance your cannon with.
	- **Agonizing Blasts:** When you cast Eldritch Blast with your Cannon, you add your spellcasting ability modifier to the damage rolls.
	- **Crippling Blasts:** Once on each of your turns when you damage a creature with an Eldritch Blast using your Cannon, you can force it to make a Strength saving throw against your spell save DC or fall [[Prone]] and have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your spellcasting modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
	- **Eldritch Javelin:** Eldritch blast attacks you make with your Cannon gain the weapon special feature of javelins, [[Pilum]].
	- **Reckless Fire:** Your shots are like a rain of lead. When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an Eldritch Blast you cast with your Cannon, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2.
	- **Lance of Lethargy:** Once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with your Eldritch Blast using your Cannon, you can reduce that creature's speed by 10 feet until the end of your next turn.
	- **Eldritch Grasp:** You can use Eldritch Blast in a special way, instead of its normal range, you can fire the blast from your cannon at a target within 30 feet, and use the Tool Mastery feature of the [[Climbers Gear]], [[Hook, line, sinker]]. You can use this feature a number of times that equals your proficiency bonus.
	- **Consuming Shot:** When you kill a creature with an Eldritch blast you casted from your Cannon, you can trigger [[Heretic's Hunger]]. This can only happen once per combat encounter.
