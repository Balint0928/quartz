The warlock is infused with the special power to absorb magic and Aether to fuel their own spellcasting. You regain one spent spell slot whenever one of the following effects occur.

- The Warlock succeeds on a saving throw against a spell an enemy controls.
- The Warlock kills a creature that is capable of casting spells, or has any AP, using a spell of any level.
- The Warlock uses their [[Aether Channel]] feature.
