You gain effects based on your current AP:

| AP  | Effect                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2   | You can make an extra attack, when you take the attack action                                                                                       |
| 4   | The first time you reach this level, you immediately gain the action effect of a [[Maestro]] song. This can only trigger once per long rest.        |
| 6   | When you take the attack action, you can also cast a spell with a casting time of 1 action, and at maximum 1st level as part of this attack action. |
