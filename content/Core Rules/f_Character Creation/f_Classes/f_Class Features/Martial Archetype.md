*Select one of the following Fighter subclasses:*

### Champion

- When you use second wind, you gain the amount you rolled on the 1d10 to weapon attack damage rolls until the start of your next turn.

- You also gain [[Weapon Mastery]] with a weapon type of your choice.
- Your crit threshold is reduced by 1

### Knight

- When you use [[Second Wind]], you can instead of regaining HP, distribute twice the amount as temporary HP to creatures you can see within 20 feet of you other than yourself.

- You gain Heavy Armor Proficiency

- You also gain [[Armor Mastery]] or Shield Mastery

### Mercenary

- You can use Second Wind an extra time per short rest.

- You can drink potions as a bonus action, instead of an action.

- You gain [[Weapon Mastery]] OR [[Armor Mastery]]

### Cavalier

- When you use Second Wind, a mount you are currently riding also regains the same amount of HP.

- Gain proficiency in *Animal Handling*. If you are already proficient or gain proficiency again later, gain the [[Skill Mastery]] for it.

- You have advantage on saving throws made to avoid falling off your mount. If you fall off your mount and descend no more than 10 feet, you can land on your feet if you're not [[Incapacitated]].

- Mounting or dismounting a creature costs you only 5 feet of movement, rather than half your speed.

- Gain the Mounted Combat [[Fighting Style]]. You can use your Constitution instead of your Wisdom modifier for the Animal Handling check used for this fighting style.
