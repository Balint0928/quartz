When you cast spells, use magic or interact with the arcane on certain levels, your body begins to accrue Aether Points (AP). 

When you cast a spell you gain an amount of AP that equals the spell's level. Overuse of magic will build up as residual Aether within you, until your body can take it no longer.

High saturation of Aether interferes with the natural balance, leylines and other ambient magic, possibly causing dangerous effects or spawning destructive creatures such as daemons, fey or aberrations. 

If your AP level reaches your threshold, which is by default 10 for all creatures, Aether explodes out of you and causes an Aether Event to take place. (Think similar to wild magic but way worse.) When this event triggers, reduce your AP to 9, resolve the Aether Event, and gain an amount of [[Exhaustion]] that equals the AP you accrued over the threshold (for example, if you had 8 AP, casted a 3rd level spell that sent you to 11 AP, that is 1 over the threshold, so gain 1 [[Exhaustion]])

You lose 3 AP after finishing a [[Long Rest]]
You lose all AP after finishing an [[Extended Rest]]
