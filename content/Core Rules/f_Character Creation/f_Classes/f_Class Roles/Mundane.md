You gain the following features if your character possesses the mundane role at the indicated levels:

**Level 1:**
- Tool Proficiency, [[Improved Interaction]]

**Level 2:**
- [[Tool Mastery]]

**Level 3:**
- Proficiency with an Exotic Weapon of your choice and Heavy armor

**Level 4:**
- [[Class Feat]] or [[Role feat]]

**Level 5:**
- [[Tool Mastery]]

## Mundane Role Progression

You gain the following if your character possesses Mundane Role Progression from their Prestige Class.

**Level 6**
- [[Artificial Potency]]

**Level 7**
- [[Tool Mastery]]

**Level 8**
- [[Advanced Mundane Role Feat]]

**Level 9**
- [[Incredible Efficiency]]

**Level 10**
- [[Ingenuity]]
