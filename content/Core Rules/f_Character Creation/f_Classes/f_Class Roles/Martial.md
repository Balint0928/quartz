 You gain the following features if your character possesses the martial role at the indicated levels:

**Level 1**
- [[Fighting Style]], Proficiency with [[Martial Knowledge]] checks, Proficiency with martial weapons

**Level 2**
- [[Weapon Specialization]], [[Provoke]]

**Level 3**
- [[Battle Hardened]]

**Level 4**
- [[Class Feat]] or [[Role feat]]

**Level 5**
- [[Extra Attack]]

## Martial Role Progression

You gain the following if your character possesses Martial Role Progression from their Prestige Class.

**Level 6**
- Additional [[Fighting Style]]

**Level 7**
- [[Advanced Fighting Style]]

**Level 8**
- [[Advanced Martial Role Feat]]

**Level 9**
- [[Battle Acumen]]

**Level 10**
- [[Improved Extra Attack]]
