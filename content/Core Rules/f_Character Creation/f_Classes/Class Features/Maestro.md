As a bard, you can use your performances to manipulate the ambient Aether inside others. This feature calls these Songs, but you are free to reimagine what these performances are.

You learn one Song that you can perform during a [[Short Rest]]. Each song also has an "in combat" section, by default you cannot use these features unless you have special access to them from another source.

- **Song of War:** If you play this song, all allies within 30 ft gain +1d4 to their attacks for the first three rounds of the next combat. If you use this as an action, the effect lasts for 1 minute, and the bonus is +2.
- **Song of Rest:** If you play this song, all allies within 30ft maximize their hit die rolls to heal themselves during a short rest. If you use this song as an action, allies within 30 feet can expend their hit dice as if they short rested as a reaction.
- **Song of Glory:** If you play this song, all allies within 30ft gain +2 to their saving throws for the first three rounds of the next combat. If you use this as an action, allies within 30 ft can reroll any saving throw against an ongoing effect currently affecting them.
- **Song of Mending:** If you play this song, all allies within 30ft can restore lost AV points that equals your proficiency bonus. If you use this as an action, allies within 30 ft can roll a number of AV dice equal to your proficiency bonus without expending them, and gaining temp HP equal to the rolled amount.
