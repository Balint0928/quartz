You must choose an Invocation below, this will augment your abilities.

### Invocation of Shadows
You gain:
- Skill proficiency with Stealth
- Tool proficiency with the [[Thieves' Tools]]
- Once per long rest, as an action, you can gain the benefit of the Invisibility spell. This doesn't count as spellcasting, but you still must maintain concentration on the spell as if on a spell.

### Invocation of Masks
You gain:
- Skill proficiency with Persuasion
- Tool proficiency with [[Disguise Kit]]
- Once per long rest, as an action, you can gain the benefit of the Disguise Self spell. This doesn't count as spellcasting.

### Invocation of Rituals
You gain:
- Skill proficiency with Religion
- Whenever you learn spells, you can also choose to learn a ritual spell that you can cast from the spell list of another class based on your spellcasting ability. 
  Charisma: Bard, Intelligence: Wizard, Wisdom: Cleric.
- Learn one ritual spell and add it to your spell list.

### Invocation of Eyes
You gain:
- Proficiency with Perception
- You can use your spellcasting ability modifier instead of wisdom for your perception checks.
- You can understand any written language.
- You gain Low light vision in a range of 30 feet. If you already have Low Light Vision, it becomes Dark Vision.

### Invocation of Stars
You gain:
- Proficiency in History
- Whenever you are restored HP by another creature, you can add your Charisma modifier to the healed amount. This can only trigger once per short rest per creature.
- Once per long rest, you can gain the benefit of the Mage Armor spell as an action. When you do so, the Dodge Value it provides increases by 1. This doesn't count as spellcasting.
