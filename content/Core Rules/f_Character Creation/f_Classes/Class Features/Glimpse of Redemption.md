When a creature hits an ally with an attack, and both are within 15 feet of you, you can use your reaction, to sway the attacker. The attacker can choose one:
- Choose a new target for the attack. If no other suitable target is present, the attack is wasted.
- The target gains resistance against the triggering damage that equals your level. Then, the target is immediately affected by the [[Provoke]] ability.
This feature can be used a number of times each combat that equals half your prof bonus.
