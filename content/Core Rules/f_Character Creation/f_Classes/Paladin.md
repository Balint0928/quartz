## Basic info:

- Role: [[Magical]] & [[Martial]]
- Hit dice: d10
- HP at 1st level: 10 + CON 
- HP beyond 1st level: 1d10(6) + CON

**Proficiencies:**
- **Armor:** Light Armor, Medium Armor, Shields
- **Weapons:** Simple Weapons, Martial Weapons
- **Save:** WIS & CHA
- **Tools:** One [[Tools and Masteries|Tool]]
- **Skills:** One [[Skills]]

# Features by level
**Level 1**
- [[Aetherial Arms]], [[Sacred Oath]]

**Level 2**
- [[Oathbound Vigilance]], [[Spell Selection]] - You only learn 1 additional spell

**Level 3**
- [[Paladin Conviction]]

**Level 4**
- [[Ability score improvement]] or [[Feat Choice]]

**Level 5**
- [[Aura of Conviction]]


## Spellcasting info
You have a list of known Paladin spells. During each [[long rest]], you can prepare a number of Paladin spells that equals your Paladin level + Charisma modifier. A spell you prepare remain prepared until you prepare something else in its place. You can only cast spells that you have prepared.
