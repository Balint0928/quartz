# Step-by-step
- First, you must choose a [[Classes#Base Classes|Class]], and note down the Role(s) your class belongs to.
- Then, pick an [[Ancestries|Ancestry]]. Take note of your Role's ancestral passives, and your lineage feature.
- For your background, choose one of the [[Regions]]. You gain the role passives for your region based on your Class roles.
- Select a [[Stat Array]] to determine your character's starting ability scores. 
- Take note of your [[Skills]]. Each character gains an additional proficiency in a skill of their choice (excluding [[Martial Knowledge]] or [[Arcane Knowledge]])
- Items depend on starting wealth, which is story dependent.
