# [[Human]]
[[Endless Determination]]: +1 to any ability score of your choice. You can add half your level to any death saving throw you make.

# [[Dwarf]]
[[Steel Skin]]: +1 to CON. When you roll AV to reduce damage, you can double your constitution for the sake of damage reduction.

# [[Elf]]
[[Ageless Grace]]: +1 to CHA. All melee Weapons you wield gain the Versatile trait. Melee attack spells gain the Finesse property.

# [[Orc]]
[[Ancient Rage]]: Your permanent AP increases by 2. Whenever you trigger [[Relentless Endurance]], you may as a reaction, make one attack, cast a spell that uses an action or take the Dodge action.

# [[Half-blood]]
[[Blended Blood]]:
- You gain an additional use of your [[Versatile Warfare]], [[Versatile Magic]] or [[Versatile Mastery]] an additional time per their respective rests.
- [[Versatile Backpack]] increases to 4 [[Bulk]]. extra carrying capacity.
- You gain the lineage feature of your [[Mixed Blood]].

# [[Halfling]]
[[Boundless Joy]]:
- Gain +2 permanent AP
- Your [[True Luck]] feature now affects all allies within 10 feet of you.
- This does not affect other halflings

# [[Eskhal]]
[[Broodmaster]]:
- You can cast the spell [[Infestation]] at will. If you don't have the [[Spellcasting]] feature, you use your Constitution for the Save DC.
- When you cast this spell, it is not considered as spellcasting. 
- When you cast this spell as an action, the damage increases to 1d8 and you can target up to an amount of targets that equals your proficiency bonus.
- When you are hit with an attack, and the attacker is within 15 feet of you, you can cast [[Infestation]] as a reaction, targeting that creature.

# [[Nekotar]]
[[Nine Lives]]:
- You gain the ability to transform into a Cat at will as an action.
	- While you are in this form, you retain all mental attributes and you can speak normally. You cannot activate or benefit from magic items, and any equipment you are wearing or carrying molds into your new form. If you are capable of casting spells, you can only cast spells that doesn't have Material or Somatic components. You can speak with and understand cats and other felines. You revert back to your normal form if you are reduced to 0 HP or as a bonus action.
- If you would die, either due to [[Exhaustion]] or instantly from massive damage, instead you are immediately transformed into your Cat form. You remain in this form until either you reduce your [[Exhaustion]] to 0 or you finish an [[Extended Rest]]. You can use this feature 9 times. Each time the feature is used, the character ages rapidly and reduces their maximum [[Exhaustion]] by 1.

# [[Itzcali]]
[[Coral Growth]]:
- Gain the ability to summon a Coral Growth, once per [[Short Rest]]. When you summon the Coral, choose one:
	- You summon the Coral as an action, in an unoccupied space within 20 feet. The Coral occupies a 5 foot cube, and provides full cover. It has 20 HP and an AV of 5. The coral remains for a minute.
	- You summon the Coral as an action targeting a medium or smaller hostile creature within 20 feet. The target must succeed a strength saving throw (DC=8+prof+CON) or become [[Restrained]] by the coral until the end of your next turn.
	- You summon the Coral as a bonus action, targeting an ally or willing creature within 20 feet. The creature is encased in the Coral until the end of your next turn. While in the coral, they are behind full cover, [[Incapacitated]], and can only use their action to spend and roll Hit Dice to recover HP. When they do so, they can add your Constitution modifier to the roll as well.
