You are under the effect of [[Sudden Death]] all the time. However, while you are conscious and not in the [[Dying]] condition, you can use your reaction to allow a creature who failed a Death Saving throw to reroll their save within 100 feet of you. A creature that succeeds thanks to this feature of yours regains 1 HP.
