Creatures that were touched by the energies and magics of other planes. 

# Lineages

## Aasimar
Aasimars are influenced by the forces of law and order. They have been touched by the power of the Heavens, Angels and Lawfully aligned Gods.


**Lineage feature**
Gain Resistance to Radiant damage that equals your proficiency bonus.

## Tiefling

Tieflings are influenced by the forces of chaos. They have been touched by the power of the Hells, Devils, and Chaotically aligned Gods.

**Lineage feature**
Gain Resistance to Fire or Necrotic damage (your choice when you create your character) that equals your proficiency bonus.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

## Genasi
Genasi are influenced by the forces of the elements. They have been touched by one of the primal forces of nature, the Elderwild or a nature inclined diety.

## Water Genasi
**Lineage feature**
Gain Resistance to Cold damage that equals your proficiency bonus.

## Earth Genasi
**Lineage feature**
Gain Resistance to Acid damage that equals your proficiency bonus.

## Fire Genasi
**Lineage feature**
Gain Resistance to Fire damage that equals your proficiency bonus.

## Air Genasi
**Lineage feature**
Gain Resistance to Thunder or Lightning damage (your choice when you create your character) that equals your proficiency bonus.
