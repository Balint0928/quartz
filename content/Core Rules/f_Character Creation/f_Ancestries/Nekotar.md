Nekotar are a species of humanoid creatures with faint feline features, large ears, long tails, sharp claws and teeth, keen senses and a bold adventurous attitude. Nekotars can easily pass off as humans, and are the most populous in the desert regions of Iqor. Nekotar trace their origins to ancient times, when humans first appeared on Iqor. Their legends say that a group of humans, devout followers of a deity/aspect called Renenet. This feline goddess, is said to have blessed her followers with cat like features.

Nekotar tend to live in tribes, with strongly matriarchal societies. Female Nekotar are usually the leaders, warriors and protectors of the tribe, while males care for the young, cook food, build tools etc… Most Nekotars still highly revere Renenet, while many have left their tribal traditional lives, to live amongst other races, and cultures.

There are two main lineages in Nekotar culture. Keepers and Seekers

# Lineages

## Seeker Nekotar
Seekers have a more agile and athletic build. Their senses are honed to find and seek prey and adventure alike.


**Lineage feature**
You gain dark vision in a range of 30 feet, and proficiency in the Athletics or Acrobatics skill

## Keeper Nekotar
Keepers are known to be more curious, hungry for knowledge. They are also characterized by thicker body hair and more pronounced feline features.


**Lineage feature**
Gain proficiency with an extra skill and Tool of your choice.
