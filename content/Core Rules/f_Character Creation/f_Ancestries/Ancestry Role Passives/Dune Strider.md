Sand cannot reduce your movement speed, and any difficult terrain made of sand imposes no penalty to you, instead they increase your movement speed by 10 feet. Furthermore, you and allied creatures (maximum 6) within 60 feet of you leave no tracks in sand, and creatures trying to track you or them in the desert have disadvantage on their checks.
