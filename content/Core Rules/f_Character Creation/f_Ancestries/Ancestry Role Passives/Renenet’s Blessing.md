You are naturally empowered by certain magics. Whenever you would be affected by or cast a spell or magical effect that grants you or a target temporary hitpoints, increases movement speed or allows a reroll for a d20 roll, you ignore all difficult terrain until the end of your next turn, and your movement cannot trigger movement based reactions.
