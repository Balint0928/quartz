You have a special natural weapon like sharp claws, piercing mandibles or a stinger. Choose one when you obtain this feature. Claws function as a [[Sickle]], Mandibles function as a [[Dagger]] and Stinger functions as a [[Spear]]. Except none of these weapons have the [[thrown]] property, their [[heft]] is 0, and you always have them drawn.
