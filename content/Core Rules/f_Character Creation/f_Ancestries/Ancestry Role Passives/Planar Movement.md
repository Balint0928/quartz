Depending on your lineage you gain the following benefit:

- **Aasimar**: You gain angelic wings that grant you a flying speed of 30 feet.
- **Tiefling**: You gain infernal wings that grant you a flying speed of 30 feet.
- **Water Genasi**: You gain swimming speed of 60 feet.
- **Fire Genasi**: Your walking speed increases by 5 feet, and if you take fire damage, this bonus doubles until the end of your next turn.
- **Earth Genasi**: You gain burrow speed of 10 feet. You can only burrow in loose earth and unworked stone.
- **Wind Genasi**: You are able to fly on the wind, gain a flying speed of 30 feet.
