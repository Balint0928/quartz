Gain proficiency with either [[Poisoner's Supplies]] or [[Healers Kit]]. If you already had proficiency with one of them, you gain an additional benefit:
- [[Poisoner's Supplies]]: Any checks made to identify poisons you crafted have their DCs increased by 2
- [[Healers Kit]]: When you heal a creature with your [[Healers Kit]], increase the amount healed to 1d8

If you would later gain proficiency in one of these tools, you also get this benefit. (You can only have one of these benefits)
