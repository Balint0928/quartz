You have special glands that create poison. You can hold proficiency bonus unit of poison in your gland, and it recharges 1 on a short or all on a [[long rest]]. You can either apply this poison to a weapon as usual, or use it immediately with your [[Insectoid Weapon]]. This is a basic poison.
