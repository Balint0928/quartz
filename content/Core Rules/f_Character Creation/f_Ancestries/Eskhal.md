Eskhal are a race of sentient insectoid people. The Eskhal’s origins are a mystery, but many attribute their creation to the same strange phenomenon that created the continent’s unique megafauna of insects.

Eskhal come in many shapes and sizes, and are usually social, community seeking creatures. There is an ancient saying in one of the Eskhan dialects: “ Yeak oiskeali tane, mardeh esteh” (A lone Eskhal is a dead one). Some scholars believe that Eskhal are the oldest sentient creatures of Iqor, and as such many of them see themselves as the natives of the continent.

Most other races view them as monstrous and barbaric, Eskhal find themselves unwelcome in many societies. In others Eskhal are kept as slaves or second class citizens.

Eskhal also have a deep kinship of all kinds of insects, and look at themselves as custodians, elevated brothers of these creatures.

# Lineages

## Aziet
These Eskhals are usually lighter framed insectoids capable of flight.


**Lineage feature**
**Insectoid Wings:** You have wings capable of flight. These wings grant you a flying speed of 20 feet. If your wings would get wet, you cannot use them until you spend 10 minutes to dry and clean them. Additionally, you can gain a point of [[Exhaustion]] to increase your flying speed to 40 feet until the end of your turn as a free action.

## Chirak
These Eskhals are usually bulkier with a heavy chitinous exoskeleton protecting their bodies.


**Lineage feature**
**Chitinous Carapace:** Your body is covered in a layer of chitin. You cannot wear armor, instead you have a special natural Medium armor. Additionally, this armor regains an AV at the end of a short or [[long rest]] where you consume an extra unit of ration.

## As'tuq
These Eskhals have many limbs and appendages.


**Lineage feature**
**Multilimbed:** You have an extra set of limbs, that allow you to choose one of the following benefits:

- Your walking movement speed increases by 5 feet, and you gain a climbing speed that equals your walking speed
- Your extra set of hands allow you to hold 2 more items/weapons. When you use an off-hand attack, you can add your ability modifier to the attack and damage roll. And you can add +1 to your total [[Heft]] if you use all your extra limbs to wield one weapon.
