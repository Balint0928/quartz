A strange race who were once slaves and servants to an ancient water dwelling empire, the Itzcali are now free to roam the seas and dry lands of Iqor. Their looks may vary greatly, ranging from hulking, coral adorned ones, to nimble merfolk. They long have fueled many tales of wonders amidst sailors. 

# Lineages

## Shrine Guardian
These Itzcali, have their humanoid form adorned and mostly made out of corals and underwater flora. They were once guardians of ancient temples and shrines, but now they are the guardians of the freed Itzcali people.


**Lineage feature:**
- [[Coral Shell]]
You have 2 AV (d8) that regrows when you finish a short or [[long rest]], and consume at least 1 unit of rations. This AV is separate from your armor's [[Armor Value]]

## Wave Shepherd
These Itzcali have have webbed hands and feet, small fins on their calves, and coloration that favors blues and greens. They also bear many physical characteristics of sea dwelling creatures. Wave Shepherds are the most well known lineage of Itzcali.


**Lineage feature:**
- [[Friend of the Sea]]
You can communicate with any Beast that has a swimming speed. Additionally, you know the Shape Water cantrip.

## Shore Stalker
Shore Stalkers are a terrifying lineage. Most of them monstrous, imposing creatures, that stalk the shores and waves, preying on sailors and anybody who trespasses into their domain. Originally created and kept as vicious soldiers, after they broke free, Shore Stalkers follow only one master. Blood.


**Lineage feature:**
- [[Watery Camouflage]]
While submerged in water, or obscured by rain, mist or fog you have advantage on stealth checks, and creatures have disadvantage on perception checks against you.
