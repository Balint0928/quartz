You have wings capable of flight. These wings grant you a flying speed of 20 feet. If your wings would get wet, you cannot use them until you spend 10 minutes to dry and clean them. Additionally, you can gain a point of [[Exhaustion]] to increase your flying speed to 40 feet until the end of your turn as a free action.
