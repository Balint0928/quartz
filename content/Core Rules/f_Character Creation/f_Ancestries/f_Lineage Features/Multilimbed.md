You have an extra set of limbs, that allow you to choose one of the following benefits:
- Your walking movement speed increases by 5 feet, and you gain a climbing speed that equals your walking speed
- Your extra set of hands allow you to hold 2 more items/weapons. When you use an off-hand attack, you can add your ability modifier to the attack and damage roll. And your strength counts as +1 higher for calculating [[Heft]] if you use all your extra limbs to wield one weapon.
