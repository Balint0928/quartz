title: Charge Action
draft: false
tags:

  - action
---
When you take this action, you can move up to an amount of feet that equals your basic movement speed + 5 x CON or DEX modifier amount. This movement cannot be broken up, and if you stop during your charge, your charge is considered finished. At the end of your charge, you can make a [[Light Attack]] against a creature within range of your weapon or unarmed attack. (This doesn't count as the [[Attack Action]], so features like [[Extra Attack]] cannot trigger.)
