You can become a whirling dancer of death as a free action at the start of your turn. While in this dance, you can make an extra melee attack each turn you take the [[attack action]] and you gain a +1 bonus to your attack and damage rolls and to your DV for each subsequent successful hit (these bonuses last until the end of your dance and can stack up to an amount that equals your DEX or STR modifier).
 
When you make an attack, you must move 5 feet before being able to attack again. You cannot move into a space you already occupied in this turn, and you cannot end your turn in the same space where you started it. 

However, if you would miss an attack or would be made prone or immobile in any way, your dance ends and you lose all benefits. 

You may only perform this dance wielding slashing weapons. You cannot perform this dance while wearing medium or heavy armor or while wielding a shield. While dancing, you cannot use features and abilities that involve concentration or requires you to stay still such as hiding. 

You may enter this dance a number of times per long rest that equals your proficiency bonus.
